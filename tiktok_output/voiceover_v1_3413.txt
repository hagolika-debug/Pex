In 1910, a daring prank fooled the Royal Navy.[1s] A group of young pranksters disguised as Abyssinian princes. They applied blackface makeup, turbans, and exotic robes. A telegram announced Prince Makalen of Abyssinia arriving at Weymouth. The navy readied an honor guard, even raising a Zanzibar flag.[0.5s] The impostors toured HMS Dreadnought, leaving officers bewildered. When uncovered, they endured a symbolic thrashing by junior officers.[1s] The scandal embarrassed the fleet, sparking nationwide laughter. Now the Dreadnought Hoax lives on as quirky Edwardian lore.
