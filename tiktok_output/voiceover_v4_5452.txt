December 13, 1867, a quiet London street erupted in chaos. The Irish Republican Brotherhood plotted a daring[0.5s] prison break. They aimed to free Ricard O’Sullivan Burke and Joseph Casey. They overloaded gunpowder, expecting a modest blast.[1s] The explosion shattered everything. Nearby homes collapsed, dozens injured, the city reeled in shock. Londoners fled the smoke,[1s] fearing a wider terror wave. The government cracked down, arresting many Irish activists overnight. The outrage sparked stricter security, but faded from textbooks. A forgotten blast that reshaped London’s fight against terror.
