In 1910, a silent war ignited in Dubai's sunrise.[0.5s] Dubai thrived as a free port, bustling with traders. Beneath the market, gun‐running arteries fed the Gulf's conflicts. Britain demanded a search for hidden weapons, threatening force. The ruler hesitated; British patience grew dangerously thin. At dawn, HMS Hyacinth's marines stormed the town.[1s] Citizens opened fire, turning quiet streets into fierce battlegrounds. The ship unleashed high‐explosive shells, razing homes and lives.[1.5s] Thirty‐seven townspeople died, reshaping Dubai's future forever.
