In 1900, Ghana's Ashanti kingdom rose against British rule. The British governor ordered the capture of the Golden Stool, Ashanti's soul. Yaa Asantewaa, the queen mother, answered:[0.5s] 'We will fight!' She rallied 20,000 warriors, including women, to defend the sacred throne. British troops fortified a tiny stockade in Kumasi, surrounded by ash.[1s] Rainstorm saved the defenders, allowing the British to retreat safely. A rescue column of 700 soldiers broke through the siege in June.[0.8s] The war ended with British victory, but the Golden Stool remained hidden. Ashanti were annexed, yet their spirit endures in modern Ghana. Remember Yaa Asantewaa, the fearless leader who defended her people's soul.
