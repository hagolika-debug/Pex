January 897, Rome:[1s] a dead pope rises for trial. Stephen VI orders Formosus' corpse out of the tomb. The rotting body sits on a throne,[0.5s] deacon answers for him. Accused of perjury, illegal papacy, and holding multiple sees. The dead pontiff is declared guilty,[1s] his papal vestments stripped. Stephen cuts off his blessing fingers, symbolically ending his power. The corpse is weighed, tied, and thrown into the Tiber River. The body washes ashore,[0.8s] miracles claimed, sparking a public uprising. Later synods nullify the trial, banning posthumous judgments forever.
