In 1864, New York hosted a grand charity fair for soldiers. Organizers commissioned a unique eagle wreath, woven entirely from human hair.[0.5s] They begged President Lincoln for a lock of his hair. Lincoln obliged, sending his hair to become the eagle’s majestic head. Locks from vice president, senators, and wives formed the wings and tail.[0.5s] The Hairy Eagle dazzled crowds, raising funds for the U.S. Sanitary Commission. But the eagle never reached the White House, disappearing after the fair. Where is it now?[1s] Hidden in a Syracuse museum, rarely displayed. A strange relic of Lincoln, still woven from his very own hair.
