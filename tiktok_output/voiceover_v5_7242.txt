Deep in Siberia, miners built a lawless gold city. Between 1883 and 1886, Zheltuga thrived like a Wild West. Twelve thousand prospectors from Russia, China, and beyond gathered. They forged a flag, a council, even a theater.[0.5s] Gold flooded the streets, but law came with whips.[0.3s] The Qing Empire finally noticed and demanded evacuation. [1s]The soldiers arrived, razed the town, and scattered its people. Today, no trace remains but whispered legends of a golden republic.
