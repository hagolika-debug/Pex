June 1859, San Juan Islands – a disputed border between two nations. A pig rooting in an American farmer's garden was shot.[1s] Lyman Cutlar fired, ending the pig's peaceful foraging. The pig's owner, a Hudson's Bay employee, demanded $100 compensation. Both nations dispatched troops, turning a farm dispute into a standoff. Young George Pickett led 66 US soldiers to guard the island. Cannons were poised, yet no shot rang out.[0.8s] Negotiations and German arbitration finally awarded the islands to America. Today the park flies both flags, a living reminder of peace. All over a pig—could a single animal spark war?[1s]
