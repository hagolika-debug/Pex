In 1593 a Filipino guard vanished from Manila He leaned against a palace wall, closed his eyes When he opened them, he stood in Mexico City Wearing Manila uniform, he bewildered the Viceroy's guards He claimed to have traveled instantly across the Pacific[1s] Two months later a galleon arrived confirming his story The ship reported the governor's assassination exactly as he described The Inquisition investigated then released him[0.8s] Gil Pérez returned to Manila his mysterious trip recorded forever
