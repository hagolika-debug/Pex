In 1891, Inner Mongolia erupted with a hidden uprising. The Jindandao sect, a secret Han society, ignited the chaos. Hundreds of thousands of Mongols fell victim to brutal massacres. Land disputes and ethnic tension fueled the violent revolt.[1s] Rebel forces stormed banner towns, slaughtering leaders and families.[0.5s] Qing troops crossed the Great Wall, crushing rebels by December.
