In 1593, a Manila guard vanished without a trace. He leaned against a wall, closed his eyes, and[0.5s] disappeared. When he opened them, he stood in Mexico City's Plaza Mayor. Witnesses gasped at the uniform—identical to Manila's palace guards. [1s]He calmly reported a murder in Manila that no one knew. Two months later, a galleon arrived, confirming every detail. The Viceroy released him, but the mystery remained unsolved. [0.8s]Historians still debate whether it was teleportation or deception. A 1609 chronicle preserved the story, making it one of history's strangest cases.
