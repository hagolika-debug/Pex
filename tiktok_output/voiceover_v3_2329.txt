In 1930s West Virginia, a deadly secret was being tunneled deep. Union Carbide built a hydroelectric tunnel to power ferrosilicon smelters. Thousands of impoverished Black migrants answered the promise of wages. The silica rock ground into invisible dust that sliced lungs. They breathed it in.[2s]Never seeing the killer. No masks, while executives inhaled behind respirators. Over 700 workers died,[1s]their graves unmarked and forgotten. Company erased records, courts lost the cases, silence prevailed. Today, the tunnel stands as a ghost of greed.[0.5s]
