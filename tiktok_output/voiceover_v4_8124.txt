In the 1880s, a rogue gold city sprang up on the Amur River. Russian and Chinese miners illegally claimed Chinese land[0.8s], ignoring Qing authority. At its height, twelve thousand prospectors flooded the makeshift town.[1s] Miners voted leaders, drafted statutes, and even built a hospital. Gambling halls and a circus thrived, earning the nickname 'Monte Carlo.'[0.5s] In 1885 Qing forces demanded evacuation; the miners ignored the warning. January 1886 Chinese troops razed the settlement, ending the gold rush. Today, Zheltuga remains a forgotten 'California on the Amur' legend.
