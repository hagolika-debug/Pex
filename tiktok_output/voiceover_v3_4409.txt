In April 1945, a secret mission saved priceless horses. Hostau, a Czech farm, housed Nazi‐owned Lipizzaner breeding stock. Both Soviets and the SS eyed the herd for meat.[0.5s] American cavalry, led by Major Andrews, planned a daring rescue. German vets, Russian Cossacks, and Allied POWs joined the effort. They herded 350 horses across enemy lines under fire.[1s] Waffen‐SS launched two attacks, but the rescuers held firm. A Soviet tank appeared; they loaded the last foals.[0.8s] All horses reached American lines, preserving the world’s finest bloodline. The daring feat later inspired Disney’s ‘Miracle of the White Stallions’.
