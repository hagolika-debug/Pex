On May 23, 1822, Oulu's quiet night erupted in flames. A blacksmith's house sparked a blaze that quickly devoured wooden homes. Within hours, three hundred thirty structures vanished, leaving only ash.[1s] The cathedral's wooden frame burned, sparing only its stone skeleton. Only five lives were lost,[0.5s] yet over three thousand were homeless. The disaster forced city planners to redesign Oulu with a modern grid. Architect Carl Engel drafted the new layout, completed by 1825. Today, Oulu's skyline hints at that fiery past,[1s] resilient and renewed.
