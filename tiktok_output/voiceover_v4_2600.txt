In the 1880s, a gold rush birthed a secret frontier city. Nestled on the Amur River, miners forged the Zheltuga Republic. At its peak, twelve thousand prospectors lived like a Wild West town. Rivers ran gold, taverns thrived, and a black‐yellow flag fluttered proudly. Law was harsh: whips for theft, bans on women, and constant vigilance. The Qing Empire finally noticed.[1s]They sent troops to end the dream. In January 1886, Chinese soldiers razed the settlement to ash. Survivors scattered, but the legend of the ‘California on the Amur’ lives. A forgotten republic, hidden in history’s shadows.[0.8s]Remember Zheltuga.
