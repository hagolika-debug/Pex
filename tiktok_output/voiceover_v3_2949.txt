In 1877, West Texas erupted over a humble white crystal. Salt from the Guadalupe Mountains fed families, preserving meat and life. When the state allowed private claims, locals felt their heritage stolen. Tejanos formed juntas, demanding communal ownership against Anglo entrepreneurs. Rangers entered town, only to be surrounded.[1s]Surrender was inevitable. The Rangers laid down guns.[0.8s]History records the only Ranger surrender. Hundreds fled to Mexico, and San Elizario lost its county seat. The 9th Cavalry arrived, restoring order but not justice. Today, the Salt War reminds us: resources can ignite revolutions.
