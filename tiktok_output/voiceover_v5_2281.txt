In 1873, a German healer sparked a deadly cult in Brazil. Jacobina Mentz claimed visions, convincing dozens to follow her mystical sermons.[1s] Neighbors called them 'Muckers', fearing their radical apocalyptic predictions. Violent clashes erupted; homes burned as the Spotters hunted the believers.[0.5s] The army intervened, surrounding Jacobina’s farmhouse on a scorching July day. Flames engulfed the walls; dozens perished, including children, in terror.[2s] Jacobina escaped with her infant, fleeing into the dense Brazilian forest. Soon after, soldiers tracked the fugitives, ending the revolt with brutal executions. Today, the Muckers’ tragedy remains a haunting reminder of fanaticism.
