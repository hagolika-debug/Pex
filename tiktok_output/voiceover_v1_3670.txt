December 1944, Allied troops await Christmas in the English Channel. The 66th Infantry Division boarded the Belgian liner Léopoldville, unaware of danger. German U‐boat U‐486 stalked the convoy, waiting for the perfect moment. At 5:54 p.m.[1s], a torpedo struck Léopoldville’s starboard side. Chaos erupted; soldiers formed lines, waiting for orders that never came.[0.8s] Lifeboats launched late; many men plunged into icy Channel water. The ship sank slowly, claiming 763 American lives, the deadliest U‐boat loss. The Army silenced survivors, censoring letters for decades.[1.2s] Declassified in 1959, the tragedy finally emerged, yet remains unknown. Remember the forgotten heroes of the Léopoldville, lost on Christmas night.
