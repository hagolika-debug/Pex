In 1932, Australia declared war... [1s] against emus. Farmers were losing crops to massive, fast‐running birds. The government sent soldiers with rifles and a machine gun. But emus scattered, outrunning bullets[0.8s], making a chaotic showdown. After weeks of futile attempts, the army withdrew defeated. The media mocked the 'war', calling it a comical disaster. Farmers finally built fences, ending the feathered conflict. Today[1.5s], the Emu War remains a bizarre reminder of nature’s stubbornness.
