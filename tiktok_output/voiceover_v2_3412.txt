In 1873 Brazil, German settlers faced a deadly religious uprising. Led by Jacobina Mentz, the 'Muckers' claimed divine visions. Neighbors called them heretics; tensions sparked violent raids and arson. The revolt erupted, blood staining the fields.[1s] On July 19, 1874, troops set Jacobina's home ablaze.[0.5s] Dozens died; Jacobina escaped briefly, only to be captured. Her gruesome fate shocked the colony forever.[0.8s] The Muckers vanished, but their story lingered in whispered folklore. Today, scholars study this obscure clash, a reminder of zeal's danger.
