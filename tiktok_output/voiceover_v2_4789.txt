In 1903, West Virginia’s quiet hills hid a deadly labor clash Coal miners demanded fair wages, safety, and the right to organize Company detectives and a federal posse gathered, guns loading silently.[1s] At dawn, the posse stormed Stanaford’s makeshift schoolhouse, bullets tearing through wood Three miners fell in their beds, blood soaking cold blankets.[0.8s] Mother Jones arrived weeks later, discovering shattered shacks and grieving families The jury blamed the posse, yet courts exonerated them entirely.[1.5s] Today, no marker stands; the hills keep the massacre’s secret Remember Stanaford, a forgotten fight for workers’ rights and justice
