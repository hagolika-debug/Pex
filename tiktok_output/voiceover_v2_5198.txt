In 1932, Australia declared war... on... emus.[1s] Veteran farmers struggled as 20,000 flightless birds devoured wheat. Minister Pearce authorized machine guns to protect the harvest. Soldiers opened fire, but emus scattered like armored tanks.[0.5s] Gun jammed after a dozen kills; birds outran the bullets. The army retreated, their dignity wounded and morale shattered.[2s] Farmers turned to fences, finally ending the absurd war. Today, the Emu War remains a quirky lesson about nature’s stubborn resilience.
