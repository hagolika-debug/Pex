On March 3, 1876, something bizarre fell from the sky[1s]. Farmers in Bath County, Kentucky, watched red meat rain onto their fields. Mrs. Crouch called it 'snowing meat'[0.5s], while neighbors smelled decay. Scientists examined the chunks, debating between vulture vomit and mysterious algae. Most evidence pointed to startled vultures regurgitating partially digested flesh mid‐flight. The bizarre incident made headlines across the nation, baffling readers everywhere. Today, a preserved sample rests in a Kentucky university museum, still puzzling. Every March, locals celebrate with a quirky festival honoring the raining meat. A reminder that history can be stranger than any fiction[1.2s].
