In 1832, a tiny borderland declared itself a nation. Nestled between New Hampshire and British Canada, it became Indian Stream.[0.8s] Confusing Treaty of Paris boundaries left both sides unsure where the line lay. Tax collectors from both nations pressed citizens, sparking outrage and rebellion. July 9, 1832: a constitution was drafted, proclaiming independence. They elected Luther Parker, a justice, to lead the fledgling republic. Tensions rose as New Hampshire militia marched toward the border.[1.5s] Facing armed sheriffs, the Republic surrendered and joined New Hampshire. A quirky footnote in history, now remembered by a lone historic marker.[1s]
