In 1918, America sent troops to frozen Russia's north. They called themselves the Polar Bear Expedition, battling bitter cold. 13,000 soldiers landed in Archangel, hoping to support anti‐Bolsheviks. But the armistice in Europe left them fighting a foreign civil war.[1s] Harsh winters froze their rifles; enemies surrounded them at every turn.[0.5s] On January 19, 1919, a Russian assault shattered their blockhouse. Half the platoon died, the rest fled into endless snow. When they finally left Archangel in June 1919, none remembered why. A forgotten chapter of American history, lost in frozen silence.[0.8s]
