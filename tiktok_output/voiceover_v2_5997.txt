In 1719, Spain tried to topple Britain from the Scottish Highlands. The plan: a secret fleet from Cádiz, bearing soldiers and guns. But a fierce storm—later called the Protestant Wind—smashed their ships.[1s] Only two frigates reached Scotland, anchoring at Eilean Donan castle. Spanish troops fortified the castle[0.8s], hoping Highland clans would rise. The Royal Navy arrived, bombarded the castle and captured the Spaniards. The Jacobites, demoralized, faced government forces at Glen Shiel. After three hours[0.5s], government troops prevailed, ending the 'Little Rising'. Today, the ruins stand as a reminder of a forgotten war.
