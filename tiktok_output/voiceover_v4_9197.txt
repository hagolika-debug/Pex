In 1651, a tiny island sparked an absurd war. The Dutch fleet, angry at royalist raids, declared hostilities. But the Isles of Scilly held only four square miles. Admiral Tromp ordered war, yet never fired a single cannon.[0.5s] The Dutch withdrew, and both sides forgot the conflict entirely. Centuries passed unnoticed until 1985, when a historian uncovered the secret. [2s]The Dutch embassy confirmed the war still technically existed. On April 17, 1986, a peace treaty finally ended it. [1s]335 years of peace, zero casualties—history’s strangest war.
