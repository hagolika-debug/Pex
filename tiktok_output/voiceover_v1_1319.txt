In 1900, German officers plotted a deadly betrayal in Old Moshi. Captain Kurt Johannes claimed a secret Chagga conspiracy against colonial rule. He convinced rival chief Mangi Marealle to fabricate evidence of the plot. Nineteen leaders, from Chagga, Arusha, and Meru, were seized. [1s]On March 2, the condemned stood beneath a lone tree. One by one, they climbed the rope, their fates sealed. The execution shocked the mountain, echoing fear across Kilimanjaro. [0.5s]No trial was offered; death was the only verdict. Today, the ancient tree still stands, a silent reminder of colonial cruelty.
