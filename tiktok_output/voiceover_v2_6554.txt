What if America began under a Swedish flag?[0.5s] Between 1638 and 1655, a secret colony thrived along the Delaware River. Peter Minuit, ex‐Dutch governor, led the Kalmar Nyckel to hidden shores. Fort Christina, now Wilmington, became the first permanent European town in the region. Swedish settlers introduced the log cabin, shaping America’s frontier architecture.[1s] Unlike rivals, they coexisted peacefully with Lenape tribes for years. In 1655, Peter Stuyvesant’s fleet forced surrender without a single shot. Old Swedes’ Church still holds services, a living relic of 1698.[0.8s] Descendants voted for independence, their votes tipping the balance toward liberty.
