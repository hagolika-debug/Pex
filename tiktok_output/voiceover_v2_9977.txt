In 1919, two U.S. pilots vanished over the Mexican desert. They crash‐landed after mistaking the Rio Conchos for the Rio Grande. Bandits captured them, demanding $15,000 ransom to spare their lives. [1s] The U.S. Army launched a daring cross‐border cavalry raid. Captain Leonard Matlack rode into Mexico with half the money, rescuing Lieutenant Peterson. [0.5s] He then faced an ambush, refusing to pay the rest. Matlack's bold gunshot forced bandits to flee, saving Lieutenant Davis. [2s] The incursion sparked diplomatic tension, ending after U.S. troops withdrew. A forgotten flashpoint of the Mexican Revolution, lost in history textbooks.
