In 1900, Mexico fought a hidden war in the desert. The Yaqui people, fierce defenders of their homeland, resisted colonization.[0.5s] General Lorenzo Torres led 5,000 soldiers to crush the rebellion. At dawn, gunfire erupted near Mazocoba hills.[1s] Hundreds of Yaqui women and children fell amid the chaos. The battle lasted three brutal hours endlessly.[0.8s] Over a thousand Yaqui were captured and forced into exile. The massacre faded from history, erased by official records. Today, their story resurfaces, reminding us of forgotten resistance.
