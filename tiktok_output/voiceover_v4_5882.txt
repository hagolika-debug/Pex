In July 1900, a river became a deadly battlefield.[1s] While the Boxer Rebellion raged, Russian troops guarded Blagoveshchensk. The frontier town teemed with thousands of Chinese merchants and families. Panic swept the streets as rumors of a Chinese uprising spread.[0.5s] Local officials ordered every Chinese resident to cross the Amur overnight. No boats were provided; they were forced to swim in freezing water. Hundreds drowned, some barely reached the opposite bank.[1s] The massacre was buried, omitted from textbooks for decades. Centuries later, scholars uncovered archives and revived the story.[0.5s] A silent river still holds the memory of those lost.
