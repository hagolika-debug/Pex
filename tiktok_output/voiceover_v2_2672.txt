Imagine meat falling from the sky like rain.[1s] On March 3, 1876, Bath County, Kentucky, it happened. Farmers saw chunks of beef, lamb, even deer plummeting down. Mrs. Crouch shouted, 'It's snowing meat on my porch!' Scientists guessed vulture vomit, cyanobacteria, even cosmic meat.[0.8s] DNA tests in 2004 couldn't identify the animal species. The town now celebrates an annual 'Meat Shower' festival. A preserved sample still sits in Transylvania University’s museum. A rainy day that turned into a carnivorous mystery.[1s]
