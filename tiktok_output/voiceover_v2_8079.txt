In 1830, Australian convicts crashed into isolated Japan without warning. They seized the brig Cyprus, fleeing Tasmania's penal colonies. After a perilous trek to China, they set sail east.[2s] Japanese samurai watched the foreign ship appear in foggy Mugi Cove. The crew begged for water, firewood, and spare sails.[1s] Samurai ordered fire; cannonballs[0.5s] rattled the hull, shaking convicts. A letter was exchanged, buying three more desperate days. Under darkness, the Cyprus slipped away, disappearing into the night. History finally recognized it as the first Australian vessel to reach Japan.
