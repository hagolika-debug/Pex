In 1725, a Dutch sailor faced a brutal punishment. Leendert Hasenbosch was sentenced for sodomy and abandoned alone. Only a tin kit: water, onions, rice, and a hatchet. He erected a white flag, hoping passing ships would see. Days turned to weeks; fresh water remained a distant dream. [1s] Desperate, he dug for water, tasting the bitter sea within. Hunger drove him to drink turtle blood and even his own urine. [0.8s] His journal recorded haunting visions and relentless loneliness. The English sailors found his tent, but he vanished without a trace. [1.2s] History remembers him as the gay Robinson Crusoe, a forgotten victim.
