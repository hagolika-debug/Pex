In 1934, a quiet Pennsylvania town erupted into gunfire on election night. The Bruno family, local political bosses, controlled everything from schools to banks. Democrats held a torchlit parade, daring the Bruno stronghold. Shots rang out as the crowd passed the Bruno house.[1s] Chaos erupted. Five people died instantly, two more within days. The incident made national headlines, shaking the 1934 elections. Bruno and his family were arrested, tried, and sentenced to life. He escaped prison, sparking the largest manhunt in Pennsylvania history.[0.8s] Captured in 1937, Bruno vanished from memory, a forgotten massacre.
