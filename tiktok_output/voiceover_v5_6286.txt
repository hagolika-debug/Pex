In 1932, Australia declared war... on flightless birds. Veteran soldiers were called back to protect wheat fields. The enemy? Tens of thousands of towering emus marching inland. Armed with Lewis machine guns, the army set up ambushes. First volley missed; birds scattered like bullets fleeing.[1s] Hours later, a gun jammed after just twelve kills. The emus outmaneuvered soldiers, dodging bullets as if tanks.[0.8s] After weeks of futile fire, the army withdrew defeated. The Great Emu War remains a bizarre lesson in nature’s resilience.
