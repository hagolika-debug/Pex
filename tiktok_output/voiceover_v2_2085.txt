In 1931 a cargo steamer vanished into Arctic ice, never sinking. She was the SS Baychimo, once a Hudson's Bay Company trader. Trapped by ice, the crew fled to Barrow, leaving the ship behind. A blizzard struck, and Baychimo vanished—only[1s] to reappear weeks later. Sightings turned the ghost ship into Arctic legend[0.5s] haunting sailors for decades. In 1933 explorers boarded her, trapped for ten days by a storm. The last confirmed glimpse came in 1969, when Inuit found her frozen. To this day Baychimo's final fate remains a chilling mystery. What if the Arctic still hides her rusted hull[2s] beneath the waves?
