In 1883, gold fever birthed a wild frontier republic. Russian and Chinese miners built a city on the Amur River. At its peak, twelve thousand prospectors lived like a lawless California. They raised taxes, built hospitals, theaters, even a casino. Violence was common; punishments included 500 lashes for thieves.[0.8s] In 1885, Qing officials demanded evacuation, but miners ignored. [1s]The Chinese army returned, razed the settlement, and dispersed survivors. By 1886, Zheltuga vanished, erased from maps forever. [0.5s]Today, its ghost remains in legend and forgotten photos.
