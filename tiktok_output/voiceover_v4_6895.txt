In 1863, a bizarre war ignited over... eggs.[1s] On the remote Farallon Islands, seabirds nested beneath looming cliffs. The Pacific Egg Company claimed sole rights to the massive murre eggs. Rival fishermen ignored the claim, risking the law and their lives. Yet President Buchanan had already declared the islands federal in 1859. At dawn, armed rowboats clashed; gunfire shattered the quiet sea.[0.8s] One man fell on each side, blood staining the rocky shore. The Egg Company held the islands, but federal pressure kept rising. By 1881, the government finally expelled all eggers, ending the conflict.[1.2s] A forgotten breakfast battle that proved even eggs can spark war.
