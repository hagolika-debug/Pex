In 1925, a tiny island sparked a silent war.[0.8s] Urta Tagay sits on the Amu Darya, between Afghanistan and the Soviet Union. Both nations claimed it since 1900, but borders remained blurry. In November, 340 Uzbek fighters, backed by Moscow, stormed the island. [1s]Gunfire erupted, leaving twelve dead on each side. Afghan troops rallied, pushing the invaders back across the river. [0.5s]The Red Army crossed, occupying Urta Tagay by night. International press condemned the aggression, urging a diplomatic solution. By August 1926, the island officially returned to Afghanistan, ending the conflict.
