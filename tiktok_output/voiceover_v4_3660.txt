October 1904: Russian fleet sails north, eyes on the horizon. Fog blankets the North Sea, tension crackles among the gun crews. Mistaken signals: trawlers glimmer like enemy torpedo boats. Searchlights snap on; shells roar, ripping through innocent nets.[0.5s] Two fishermen die, six wounded, a trawler sinks beneath the waves. Russian ships even fire on each other, chaos erupts.[1s] Diplomacy teeters; Britain threatens war over the accidental massacre. An international commission forces Russia to pay £66,000 compensation.[0.8s] A memorial in Hull still whispers the tragedy of Dogger Bank.
