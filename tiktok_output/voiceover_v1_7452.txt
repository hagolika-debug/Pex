In 1895, Hawaiʻi erupted in a secret royalist uprising. Former Congressman Robert Wilcox led the rebellion to restore Queen Liliʻuokalani.[0.5s] Only three days of fierce battles raged across Oahu's hills. First clash at Diamond Head saw policemen ambushed in a canoe house.[1s] Royalists captured a police lieutenant, but lost the element of surprise. Government artillery bombarded the rebels, missing Diamond Head entirely. By January 9, the uprising collapsed as rebels fled into valleys.[0.8s] Wilcox surrendered weeks later, ending the last stand for the Hawaiian monarchy. Today, this forgotten counter‐revolution fades into Hawaii’s hidden past.
