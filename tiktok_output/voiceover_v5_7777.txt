A war ignited over a single pig. In 1859, the San Juan Islands became a disputed border. American farmer Lyman Cutlar shot the pig devouring his potatoes. Both nations deployed troops and cannons,[1s] yet no shots fired. General Harney and Captain Hornby stared across the foggy strait. President Buchanan sent Winfield Scott to negotiate,[0.5s] averting war. German Emperor Wilhelm I arbitrated, awarding the islands to America. A joint military occupation lingered,[1s] now a peaceful historic park. Future Confederate hero George Pickett led troops in this quiet clash. All because of a pig—imagine the chaos if we fought over it.
