In 1904, a naval nightmare unfolded over the North Sea. The Russian Baltic Fleet raced toward Japan, fearing unseen torpedo boats. Nightfall, dense fog, nerves frayed.[1s] The crew saw phantom lights. Mistaking humble trawlers for enemy, the Russians unleashed deadly fire. Gunfire erupted, Russian ships targeting each other in the dark.[0.5s] Chaos reigned. Two British fishermen died, several wounded; a trawler sank beneath waves. The incident almost sparked war between Britain and Russia.[1.2s] Tensions boiled. An international commission forced Russia to pay £66,000 in compensation. Today, Hull's Fisherman's Memorial stands, remembering the forgotten tragedy.
