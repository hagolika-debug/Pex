On October 12, 1912, a spark ignited tragedy in Tasmania. The pump house at 700‐foot level burst into unforgiving flames. 42 miners trapped[0.5s], smoke choking every breath, hope fading fast. Rescue teams raced across Bass Strait, the SS Loongana set a record. [1s]Inside the mine, a note pinned to timber begged his wife. Another desperate message: ‘40 men in 40 stope, send food.’ [0.8s]After four days, the last survivor emerged, 100 hours later. Heroes received medals, yet the fire’s cause remained a mystery. Centennial songs now echo, reminding us of lives lost in ash.
