In 1900, a whole village vanished in a blaze. The Burt Lake Band of Chippewa and Ottawa lived peacefully on Michigan’s inland lake.[0.5s] A greedy land speculator claimed tax titles on their homes. Sheriff Ming arrived with deputies, ordered the families to leave. Then they set fire to each cabin, one by one.[1s] Only the 1838 St. Mary’s church survived the inferno. Nineteen families were forced onto nearby lands, their village erased. Decades later, the band fought in federal court for their stolen land. Today, the memory burns, reminding us of hidden injustices.
