In July 1900, a hidden mountain pass became a battlefield. After Pretoria fell, British troops marched into the Magaliesberg. Boer guerrillas slipped north, disappearing among the rugged cliffs. Koos de la Rey gathered two hundred men, plotting a surprise ambush. At dawn, they struck the Scots Greys, killing twenty‐four.[1s] They captured one‐hundred ninety‐eight soldiers, shocking the British command.[0.8s] Field Marshal Roberts censured his officers, blaming negligence for the defeat. Two weeks later, the British launched a massive pincer at Silkaatsnek.[0.5s] Though the second battle succeeded, the first revealed guerrilla warfare's power. Today, Silkaatsnek remains a hidden lesson in military history.
