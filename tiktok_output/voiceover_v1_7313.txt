In 897 Rome, a dead pope faced a courtroom. Pope Stephen VI ordered Formosus' corpse exhumed from its tomb. The body was propped on a throne, silent witnesses watching. He was charged with perjury, illegal papacy, and ambition. The dead pope could not answer.[1s] The judges whispered. Stephen VI stripped the corpse, cutting off its blessing fingers. They declared Formosus guilty, nullifying every act he performed. The body was thrown into the Tiber, floating among dead leaves. Rome erupted in outrage.[0.8s] The pope who sentenced died soon.
