July 1900, Blagoveshchensk, a Russian frontier city trembles. Boxer Rebellion erupts, Chinese forces threaten the Amur banks. Local officials panic, decide to expel every Chinese resident. They gather thousands, march them to the river's edge. Command: swim across the icy Amur, no boats provided. Shots ring out, Cossacks fire as people scramble.[2s] Most drown; only a few survive the brutal crossing. The tragedy vanishes from textbooks, remembered only in whispers.[1s] History reminds us: forgotten horrors can surface again.
