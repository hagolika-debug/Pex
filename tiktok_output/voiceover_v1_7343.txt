April 27, 1865: a Mississippi steamboat overloaded with soldiers set sail. The Sultana could legally carry three hundred, yet packed over two thousand. Greedy officials bribed the captain, ignoring safety for profit. At 2 a.m., the boiler exploded, igniting a fiery inferno.[1s] The blast killed nearly two thousand men, more than the Titanic.[0.8s] News of the disaster was eclipsed by Lincoln’s assassination.[1.2s] Families never learned the fate of their sons, buried in river mud. The tragedy faded, only a memorial stands near Memphis today. Remember the Sultana, the deadliest forgotten disaster in American history.
