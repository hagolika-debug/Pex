In 1184, noble leaders gathered for peace talks...[1s]They never left the same. Inside Erfurt’s ancient church, wooden floors creaked under heavy armor. A bitter feud between Archbishop Conrad and Landgrave Ludwig sparked the gathering. Suddenly[0.5s], the floor gave way, dropping nobles into a foul abyss. They plunged into a cesspit, drowning in rotting sewage and despair. Miraculously[1s], King Henry clung to iron rails, escaping the stench.
