In 1932, Australian wheat farmers faced an unexpected enemy. Massive flocks of emus descended, devouring crops and destroying fences. The government responded.[1s]They sent soldiers armed with machine guns. Major Meredith led the operation, hoping to thin the relentless bird army. First battle began at dawn.[0.5s]The guns jammed after a dozen kills. Emus scattered, outrunning bullets; soldiers watched the birds dodge like tanks. After weeks of futile firing, the army withdrew, humbled by flightless foes. The media mocked the 'Emu War'.[1s]The birds won the day. Today, the saga reminds us nature can outsmart even the strongest armies.
