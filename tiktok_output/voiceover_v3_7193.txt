In August 1888, a fireball split the night sky over Kurdistan. Villagers watched as glowing rocks rained down on the hills. One stone struck a shepherd, killing him instantly. Another fragment crippled a nearby farmer, leaving him paralyzed. The Ottoman governor documented the tragedy, sending reports to Istanbul.
