London, 1814: a quiet afternoon turns into a deadly wave. At Meux's Horse Shoe Brewery, a massive wooden vat gives way. Four thousand gallons of porter surge, crashing through brick walls.[1s] The torrent floods St Giles' cramped slums, drowning eight unsuspecting victims. Neighbors watch in horror as beer roars like a river. Crowds gather, some sip the spilled brew, unaware of danger.[0.5s] The disaster bankrupts Meux, but sparks a shift to steel vats. Today, the site houses the Dominion Theatre, a reminder of the foam. A forgotten tsunami of beer, forever etched in London's history.[0.8s]
