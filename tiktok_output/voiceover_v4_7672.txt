In 1903, a quiet coastal town hid a deadly secret. Robb Jetty stored thousands of dynamite boxes for distant gold mines. At 7 p.m., July 15th, a thunderous blast shattered the night. The shockwave ripped the building apart, sending fireballs skyward.[1s] Nightwatchman Thomas Whelan vanished inside, never to be seen again. Police suspected sabotage, pointing fingers at disgruntled caretaker Robert Carrick. Keys mysteriously reappeared in the dunes days later.[0.8s] The inquest declared deliberate destruction, yet no one was ever charged. Today, the ruins whisper a forgotten tale of explosive intrigue.
