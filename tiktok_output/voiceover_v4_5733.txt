In 1898, America witnessed its only successful coup.[0.5s] White supremacists overthrew Wilmington's thriving Black‐majority city government. A fiery speech warned they’d choke the Cape Fear River.[1s] Violence erupted as mobs wielded rifles and a Colt machine gun. Over sixty Black leaders were forced out, many brutally murdered. The Daily Record newspaper burned, silencing the Black community's voice. The coup erased Black political power for the next seventy‐five years. History books called it a ‘race riot,’ hiding the truth. Now the story resurfaces, demanding remembrance and justice.[0.8s]
