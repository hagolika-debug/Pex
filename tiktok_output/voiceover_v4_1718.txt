In 1184, a royal court met in Erfurt, Germany. King Henry VI gathered nobles to settle a bitter land dispute. The assembly sat above ancient cesspits beneath the cathedral hall. Heavy armor and crowded benches weakened the rotting wooden floor.[1s] Suddenly, the floor gave way, plunging everyone into darkness. Nobles fell into a foul cesspit, drowning in human waste. Around sixty aristocrats perished, including counts and bishops. King Henry survived, perched in a stone alcove above the chaos.[0.8s] The tragedy reshaped medieval gatherings, reminding power can crumble beneath filth.
