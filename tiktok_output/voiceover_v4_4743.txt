In the early 1800s, a tiny nation sprouted between New Hampshire and Canada. The cause? A blurry line on the 1783 Treaty of Paris. Both Britain and the United States sent tax collectors, sparking double taxation.[0.8s] Fed up, locals declared independence on July 9, 1832, forming the Indian Stream Republic. They drafted a constitution, electing Luther Parker as justice of the peace. For three years, they governed peacefully.[2s] Then New Hampshire militia threatened invasion, demanding the territory surrender. The assembly capitulated. The Republic vanished.[1s] Finally, the 1842 Webster‐Ashburton Treaty officially placed the land in New Hampshire.
