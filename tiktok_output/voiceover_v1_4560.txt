In 1932, Australia declared war on a feathered menace. Farmers begged for help as emus devoured their wheat fields. The Defense Minister deployed troops armed with Lewis machine guns. Soldiers fired, yet the birds scattered like unstoppable tanks.[1s] Emus split into small groups, evading every bullet effortlessly. Guns jammed, ammo dwindled, morale plummeted under relentless bird assaults.[0.8s] After weeks of chaos, the army withdrew, humbled by birds. Today, the Emu War stands as a bizarre reminder of nature's power.
