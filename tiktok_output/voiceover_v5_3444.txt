In 1873, Brazil's southern frontier birthed a deadly religious uprising. Jacobina Mentz, a charismatic healer, claimed to channel divine visions.[0.5s] Her followers, called the Muckers, clashed with conservative Spotters. In June 1874, they torched sixteen homes, sparking terror. The militia responded, surrounding Jacobina’s farmhouse at dawn.[2s] Flames devoured the home, killing men, women, and children. Jacobina escaped, cradling her infant, disappearing into the woods.[1s] The revolt ended, but its legend haunted southern Brazil for decades.
