In October 1861, Assam’s quiet villages sparked a fierce revolt. The British imposed new taxes on betel leaf and areca nut. Thousands of farmers convened a raijmel, demanding relief.[0.5s] Authorities dismissed them, sending armed police to break the assembly. When Lieutenant Singer tried to disarm them, a farmer struck his head.[1s] The British responded with gunfire, killing dozens of Assamese peasants.[0.8s] Over a hundred villagers were arrested; leaders faced execution or exile. The Phulaguri Uprising remains a forgotten spark of India's freedom struggle.
