June 16, 1883, Sunderland gathered for a children's free‐toy giveaway. The Victoria Hall theater promised the greatest treat[0.5s] ever for kids. Excitement turned to panic when the final announcement shouted: 'Toys!' Children surged down a narrow staircase toward a locked, inward‐opening door.[1s] The door wouldn't budge, crushing bodies together in a suffocating heap. One girl emerged clutching her dead sister, a haunting image forever etched. 183 young lives lost that day, prompting nationwide safety reforms.[0.8s] Laws now require outward‐opening doors and the modern push‐bar safety feature. Remembering Sunderland's tragedy reminds us: safety must never be taken for granted.
