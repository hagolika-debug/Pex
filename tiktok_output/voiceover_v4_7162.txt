Imagine a courtroom where the defendant is already dead.[1s] In 897, Rome's basilica became a macabre stage. Pope Stephen VI commanded Formosus' corpse to be dug up. The dead pope was propped on a throne, robes draped. Deacons asked, 'Why did you usurp the holy seat?' Judges declared him guilty, slicing off his blessing fingers.[0.8s] They tied weights, tossed his body into the Tiber River. Romans rebelled, later the synod was nullified, Formosus restored.[0.5s] The Cadaver Synod remains a chilling reminder of power's madness.
