The shortest war ever recorded lasted just under an hour.[1s] In August 1896, Zanzibar's Sultan Hamad died unexpectedly. His nephew Khalid claimed the throne, ignoring British protocol. Britain delivered an ultimatum: surrender by 9 am or face war.[0.8s] At 09:02, British cruisers unleashed a relentless barrage on the palace. Flames rose, artillery fell silent, and chaos erupted within minutes. By 09:40, the guns fell quiet—38 minutes of combat. The British installed Sultan Hamoud, ending the slave trade soon after.[0.5s] A war so brief, it fits in a coffee break.
