Deep in Siberia, a golden dream rose from river mud. In 1883, prospectors discovered glittering veins along the Amur’s banks. Russians and Chinese built a lawless town, calling it Zheltuga. At its peak, twelve thousand miners lived like wild pioneers.[1s] They forged their own flag, black and yellow, flashing hope. But no nation claimed them.[1s] The Qing Empire watched, uneasy. In 1885, Chinese troops finally stormed the illegal gold oasis. The settlement burned, its riches vanished, leaving only ghost stories. Today,[0.5s] Zheltuga lives in history, a fleeting glitter on the map.
