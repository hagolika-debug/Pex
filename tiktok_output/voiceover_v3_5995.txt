In 1184, nobles met for peace—under a rotting floor.[1s] Erfurt’s grand church, built on centuries‐old wooden beams. King Henry VI summoned aristocrats to settle a bitter land feud. The wooden floor creaked as hundreds gathered, weight mounting.[0.5s] Suddenly, the floor gave way—plunging them into a foul cesspit.[1s] Men drowned in sewage, others crushed, fumes stealing breaths. Miraculously, Henry clung to iron rails, escaping death. Sixty to a hundred nobles vanished; politics reshaped forever. The Erfurt Latrine Disaster still haunts medieval history. Remember: even power can crumble beneath a rotten floor.
