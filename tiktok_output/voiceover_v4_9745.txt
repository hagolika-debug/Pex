A hidden 19th‐century war erupted in Brazil's southern plains. German settlers built farms, but isolation brewed a dark religious fervor. Jacobina Mentz, a trance‐speaking healer, claimed divine visions and miracles. Her followers, called Muckers, clashed with the Spotters, the local opposition. Violent raids ignited, homes burned, families massacred, fear spread rapidly. On July 19, 1874 troops stormed their farmhouse, flames devouring everything.[2s] Survivors fled, Jacobina vanished with a newborn, only blood‐stained legends remained. Today, the Mucker Revolt is a silent scar, forgotten by most.[1s] Films and books try to resurrect this eerie chapter of Brazilian history. Will you remember the whispers of the Muckers?[0.5s]
