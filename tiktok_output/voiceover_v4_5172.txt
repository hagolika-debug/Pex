In 1815, long after the War of 1812, a tragedy unfolded. American sailors were confined at the bleak Dartmoor Prison on Devon’s moors.[0.8s] Months passed, food rations dwindled, and frustration boiled among the prisoners. On April 6th, guards ordered the men back into the courtyard. The crowd surged, voices rose, and the militia opened fire.[0.5s] Seven Americans fell dead, thirty‐one wounded, blood staining the stone.[1s] The world barely noticed; the incident was buried in history. Only centuries later did scholars uncover this forgotten massacre. Remember the Dartmoor tragedy—a silent reminder of war's lingering wounds.
