A vague 1783 treaty sparked a border that vanished. [1s] 300 settlers lived between the US and British Canada, paying double taxes. Confusion over the Connecticut River’s headwaters left no clear jurisdiction. In July 1832 they drafted a constitution, claiming self‐rule. A five‐member council, courts, and a militia of forty‐one men. [2s] A British sheriff’s arrest sparked an international skirmish. New Hampshire militia occupied the area in late 1835, ending autonomy. The 1842 Webster‐Ashburton Treaty finally settled the border. Today Pittsburg, NH, hides a story of a lost nation. Remember the Republic of Indian Stream, history’s hidden border.
