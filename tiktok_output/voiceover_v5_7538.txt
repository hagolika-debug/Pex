In 1900, a silent tragedy unfolded on the Amur River that few remember. While China fought the Boxer Rebellion, Russian forces turned inward. Blagoveshchensk, a bustling border town, teemed with Chinese traders.[1s] Lieutenant‐General Gribskiy ordered all Qing subjects expelled across the frozen river. They were forced into icy waters[0.8s], shot as they tried to swim. Over 3,000 lives vanished, and the Chinese community vanished from the region. Both Russian and Chinese governments later erased the massacre from history books. Today, descendants whisper the story, a scar on Sino‐Russian relations. Remember the forgotten victims, lest silence become forever.
