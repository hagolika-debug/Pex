On a cold February night, the sky turned into a firework.[1s] Hundreds of fireballs marched from horizon to horizon, silent and eerie. No radiant point—just a line of glowing giants crossing the heavens. Observers gasped, then fell silent.[0.8s] The world held its breath. Thunder-like booms[0.5s] echoed, shaking houses as meteors tore the night. From Canada to Bermuda, even ships near Brazil witnessed the procession. Astronomers later called it the Cyrillids, a temporary Earth satellite. Some say it was a ring of debris, still unproven. Imagine looking up, knowing the sky could dance again tomorrow.
