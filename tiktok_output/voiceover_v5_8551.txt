Imagine a bustling port, now swallowed by the sea.[1s] In the 13th century, Dunwich rivaled London’s trade size. Massive storms in 1286 and 1320 ripped churches from cliffs. Erosion ate the harbor, forcing merchants to flee forever. Locals whispered of a British Atlantis, bells ringing beneath waves.[0.8s] In 1972, diver Stuart Bacon spotted a church tower, encrusted with sponges. High‐resolution sonar in 2008 mapped medieval walls, graves, and foundations. Today, rising seas threaten the same fate for coastal towns worldwide. Will we remember the ghosts before they’re gone?[1s]
