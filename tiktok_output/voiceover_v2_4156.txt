A secret war disaster that claimed over a thousand lives. In November 1943[0.5s], a troopship crossed the Mediterranean waters. German pilots unleashed a radio‐guided glide bomb on the vessel. The blast tore a massive hole, fire devouring decks instantly. [2s]Chaos erupted as soldiers scrambled for lifeboats that wouldn’t lower. Rusted fittings jammed the davits, leaving most vessels stuck. [1s]British destroyers raced in, pulling over six hundred survivors from the sea. The U.S. Army classified the loss, silencing families for decades. Today, the Rohna tragedy reminds us how history can be buried.
