In 1832, a tiny New England pocket declared independence. The 1783 Treaty of Paris left the border fuzzy between US and Britain. Indian Stream sat between the Connecticut River headwaters and disputed lands.[0.5s] Residents grew tired of double taxes from two governments. They drafted a constitution and elected Luther Parker as justice. On July 9, 1832, the Republic of Indian Stream was proclaimed. New Hampshire militia threatened invasion; towns prepared arms.[1s] By August 5, 1835, the Republic peacefully surrendered to New Hampshire. The Webster‐Ashburton Treaty of 1842 finally settled the boundary.[0.7s] Today, Pittsburg, NH remembers its fleeting nation with a historic marker.
