Imagine convicts escaping Tasmania, sailing into forbidden Japan.[1s] In 1829, the brig Cyprus mutinied, stealing a ship from Hobart. The ragtag crew crossed the Indian Ocean, desperate for freedom. January 16, 1830, they anchored off Mugi, Shikoku, Japan.[0.5s] Samurai disguised as fishermen inspected the foreign ship, noting a foul stench. Local commander ordered cannon fire, but the ship stubbornly refused to sail.[1s] The convicts begged for water and firewood, using sign language. Eventually the samurai gave supplies, and the Cyprus slipped away under night.[0.8s] Historians now confirm it was the first Australian ship to breach Japan's isolation.
