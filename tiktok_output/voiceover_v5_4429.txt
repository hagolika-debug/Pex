April 27, 1865: A steamboat explosion eclipsed Lincoln's death.[1s] The Sultana carried over 2,000 soldiers, five times its legal limit. Patchy boiler repairs and greed set the stage for catastrophe. At 2 a.m., boilers burst, fire engulfed the night river.[0.5s] Up to 1,800 Union troops perished, making it America’s deadliest maritime disaster. News was smothered by Lincoln’s assassination and Booth’s death.[1s] Only one captain faced court‐martial, later exonerated, leaving the tragedy unpunished. Today, the Sultana rests beneath the Mississippi, its story nearly lost. Remember the forgotten heroes; their sacrifice should echo through history.
