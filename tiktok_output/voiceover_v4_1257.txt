In 1840, a fleeting nation rose on the border.[0.5s] Discontented Mexican states declared independence from Santa Anna's centralist regime. Laredo became the capital of the unrecognized Republic of the Rio Grande.[1s] Led by President Jesús de Cárdenas and General Antonio Canales. Their flag bore three stars, representing Coahuila, Nuevo León, and Tamaulipas. Texan volunteers crossed the river, fighting alongside Mexican federalists. Battles raged at Mier, Santa Rita, and Saltillo, costing many lives.[0.8s] By November, the rebellion crumbled, surrendering back to Mexico. Today, its legacy lives in a museum and a forgotten seventh flag.
