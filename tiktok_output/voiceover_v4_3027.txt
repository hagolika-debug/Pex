A tiny steamer sparked a diplomatic crisis that could've ignited war. The Virginius, built for the Civil War, now smuggled arms to Cuban rebels. In October 1873, Spanish warship Tornado seized her off Cuba's coast. Spain tried the crew as pirates, executing over fifty men in brutal fashion.[1s] America and Britain protested, demanding the crew's release and an apology. U.S. warships raced to Santiago, threatening to bombard if more killings occurred.[0.8s] Negotiations forced Spain to halt executions and eventually return the ship.[0.5s] The incident exposed U.S. naval weakness, spurring future shipbuilding programs.
