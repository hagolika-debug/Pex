In 1903, a quiet Appalachian town became a battlefield. Miners, demanding union rights, faced armed posses hired by coal barons. February twenty‐five, dawn broke over Stanaford.[1s]Gunfire erupted. Over a thousand bullets flew, houses turned into bullet‐riddled graves. Three miners died in their beds, their families wept helplessly. Mother Jones arrived, her heart broken by the carnage.[0.8s]She demanded justice. The coroner’s jury called it a massacre, yet the posse walked free. Today, no marker stands; the hills keep the silent secret.[1.2s] Remember Stanaford, the forgotten spark that ignited America’s labor wars.
