A hidden nation existed between the US and Canada for three years. Treaty of Paris left the border unclear[0.5s], sparking rivalry. British sheriffs and American tax collectors both demanded money from locals. On July 9 1832, they declared the Republic of Indian Stream. A constitution was drafted, electing Luther Parker as justice. [1s] New Hampshire militia marched, demanding surrender. By August 1835 the tiny republic was forced back into New Hampshire. The border finally settled in 1842, yet the tale endures. Today Pittsburg, New Hampshire commemorates the spot with a historic plaque.
