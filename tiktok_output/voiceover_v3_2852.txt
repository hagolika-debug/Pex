Deep in the Siberian wild, miners chased golden dreams. In 1883, a gold strike sparked a borderless town.[0.5s] Russian and Chinese prospectors built Zheltuga, the Amur’s 'California'. Population swelled to twelve thousand, a melting pot of outlaws. Lawless nights, whips cracked, and gold flowed like rivers.[1s] A black‐and‐yellow flag flew over saloons and theaters. In 1885, Qing officials demanded evacuation, threats looming. Chinese troops arrived, razed the town, and scattered survivors.[0.8s] Zheltuga vanished, but its legend whispers across the Amur.
