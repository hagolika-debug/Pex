In 1899, the gold rush town of Dawson burned to ashes. Built of green timber, the buildings were tinder waiting for spark. It started in the Bodega Saloon, April 26, flames leapt instantly. Within minutes, 117 structures vanished, consuming over a million dollars' worth. The sky turned orange, the river reflected fire.[1.5s] Witnesses saw flames devour homes, yet some rebuilt within hours. Volunteer firemen battled with hoses, chemicals, but the blaze won. Another fire struck in January 1900, sealing the town's fate.[2s] The disaster finally prompted a professional fire department, ending endless infernos.
