In 1910, the quiet island of Coloane hid a deadly secret. Pirate gang seized seventeen schoolchildren, demanding a massive ransom. Governor Eduardo Marques declared martial law, rallying troops to rescue hostages. First Portuguese landing failed[1s]; ambushed soldiers fell under fierce fire. A hundred soldiers and a gunship bombarded Coloane, turning night into chaos. By August[0.5s], rescued children reunited, while dozens of pirates were captured. Portugal suffered only two dead, a surprisingly low toll for such turmoil. The conflict cemented Portugal's claim over Coloane, despite Qing protests. Today, a stone monument stands, a silent[0.8s] reminder of forgotten battles.
