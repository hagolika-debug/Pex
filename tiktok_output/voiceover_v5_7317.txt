In 1899, Italy dared to claim a Chinese bay. The Scramble for China had already divided European powers. Italian merchants dreamed of a coal hub in Sanmen Bay. Minister Canevaro sent a bold ultimatum to the Qing.[0.8s] Beijing rejected the demand, sparking a diplomatic showdown. Britain and Germany watched, unsure whether to back Italy.[1s] Within weeks the mission faltered, Italy withdrew in humiliation. The affair marked the only time Qing refused a concession.[0.5s] Today, Sanmen Bay remains a quiet coast, unaware of the chase. History hides such battles—what other forgotten wars await?
