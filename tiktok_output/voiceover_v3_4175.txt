In 1925, a single stray dog ignited a Balkan crisis. Greek soldier chased his dog across the Bulgarian border[0.5s], triggering gunfire. Bulgarian sentries fired, killing a Greek captain and a soldier. Greece demanded apology, money, and immediate Bulgarian retaliation[1s]. Greek troops invaded Petrich, the quiet Bulgarian town, on October 22. Bulgaria appealed to the League of Nations for peace. The League ordered a ceasefire, forcing Greeks to withdraw[0.8s]. Greece paid £45,000, ending the ten‐day conflict. A stray dog sparked an international incident, reminding us of fragile borders.
