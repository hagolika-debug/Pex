1610, a lone Portuguese carrack faced a sea of samurai. Portugal's silk trade made Japan's ports a glittering prize. The clash began years earlier in Macau, where Japanese sailors provoked bloodshed. And then[1s], the black ship entered Nagasaki Bay. Captain André Pessoa ignited the powder, turning decks into blazing infernos. Samurai surged forward.[0.8s] Their swords clanged against cannon fire. The ship erupted, scattering treasure and sealing a fiery legend. Japan turned to the Dutch, ending Portuguese influence for centuries. Today, divers still chase ghostly ruins of the lost carrack. What other hidden wars lie beneath history’s quiet surface?
