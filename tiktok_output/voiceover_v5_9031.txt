July 5, 1918: A night of celebration turned tragic. Over 500 locals boarded the Columbia, dreaming of dancing. Fog rolled in like a thick blanket.[1s]Visibility vanished. The pilot struck a hidden log; the hull screamed. Decks collapsed, trapping families beneath twisted wood.[0.5s]Panic erupted. By midnight, the steamer slipped beneath the river, silent. Eighty‐seven souls lost; many were from Pekin alone. The disaster ended river excursions forever.[1.2s]A town mourned. Today, a quiet park marks the tragedy, remembering lives.
