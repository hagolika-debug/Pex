New Year's Day, 1915, remote mining town awakens to celebration. Two Afghan cameleers, Gool Mohammed and Abdullah, plot a deadly ambush. A packed picnic train carries over a thousand hopeful passengers through the outback. Gunfire erupts as the train slows; chaos erupts.[1s] Screams echo across the desert. Three civilians die, ten are wounded, including a seventeen‐year‐old girl. Locals seize rifles, chase the attackers up the rocky hills. In retaliation, the German club burns; fear fuels rising suspicion.[0.8s] Tension spreads. Today only a modest plaque marks the silent stones of Broken Hill.
