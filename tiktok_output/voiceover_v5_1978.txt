In 1325, two Italian city‐states clashed over a simple bucket. Bologna, a Guelph stronghold, demanded the prized wooden pail from Modena. [1s] The conflict escalated into the Battle of Zappolino, a massive medieval showdown. Modena’s 7,000 troops faced Bologna’s 32,000, yet the underdogs prevailed. [0.5s] The victory was celebrated by stealing a bucket from a well‐nearby. Legends claim the bucket became a trophy, symbolizing Modena’s triumph. [2s] Yet historians reveal the war was really about disputed border castles. The absurd conflict inspired a mock‐heroic epic, still taught in Italian schools. Today, the stolen bucket sits in Modena’s civic museum, a quirky relic. A bucket sparked war, proving even tiny objects can ignite massive histories.
