In 1184, noblemen met for peace—under a cursed floor. The Hoftag gathered in Erfurt's ancient church, wooden beams creaking. They whispered alliances.[1s]Then the floor gave way. A massive plunge into a foul cesspit swallowed dozens of aristocrats. Some drowned in excrement, others crushed, few gasped for air. King Henry clung to iron rails, escaping the stench and death. The nobles perished.[0.8s]History turned on a bathroom floor. The dispute remained unresolved, but Henry rose to become Holy Roman Emperor. A medieval toilet tragedy reminds us power can crumble beneath any seat.
