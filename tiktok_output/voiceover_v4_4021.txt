In 1184, a council turned into a deadly plunge. King Henry VI summoned nobles to resolve a bitter land feud. They gathered beneath the Petersberg citadel’s ancient stone church. The wooden floor, weakened by age, couldn't bear their weight. Suddenly, the floor gave way.[1s]The nobles plunged into darkness. Below, a foul cesspit swarmed with rotting sewage and fumes. Sixty to a hundred nobles drowned or were crushed.[0.8s]History shuddered. King Henry and Archbishop Conrad clung to iron rails, surviving. The dispute faded, but the deadly toilet became legend.
