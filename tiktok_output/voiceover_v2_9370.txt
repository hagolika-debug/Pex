In 1730, enslaved Africans seized a British slave ship and turned the tide. [1s] The Little George departed Guinea, bound for Rhode Island's markets. Captives cramped, chained, darkness below deck—desperation brewed. At 4 a.m., they broke chains, stormed the deck. They seized cutlasses, killed three watchmen, threatened explosion. [0.5s] Facing a bomb, the crew surrendered, fearing the ship sank. Without training, they steered the vessel back toward African shores. The Little George reached Sierra Leone River, and the captives reclaimed freedom. Later rescued crews recounted the daring revolt, preserving its story. [1.2s] This rare maritime rebellion still inspires the fight for liberty.
