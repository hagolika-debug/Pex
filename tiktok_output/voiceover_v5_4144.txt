In 1900, German colonizers staged a chilling public hanging. Nineteen Chagga, Arusha and Meru leaders faced the rope. They were accused of a fabricated[0.5s] conspiracy against the empire. Captain Kurt Johannes orchestrated the execution without a fair trial. The tree still stands, a silent witness to the tragedy. [2s]The air smelled of smoke, fear and looming death. Villagers watched helplessly as each chief ascended the gallows. [1s]The executions cemented German control over Kilimanjaro. Today, the story remains a forgotten scar in Tanzanian history.
