July 21, 1918, a quiet Cape Cod beach turned battlefield. German U‐boat U‐156 surfaced, targeting a humble tug and barges. Shells screamed across the water.[1s]The sand shivered under fire. Vacationers gathered, eyes wide, as artillery lit the horizon. Two crewmen wounded, yet the tug barely stayed afloat. A boy waved the American flag, rifle in hand.[0.8s]Hope surged. Four barges sank, but fishermen rescued crews and a loyal dog. The attack sparked nationwide panic, yet no lives were lost. History forgot the battle, but its story still echoes today.
