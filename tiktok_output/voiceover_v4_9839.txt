In 1883, a hidden gold rush birthed a secret republic on the Amur River. Russian and Chinese prospectors, plus Koreans and Jews, swarmed the riverbank. At its height, twelve thousand miners called Zheltuga home.[0.5s] They raised a black‐and‐yellow flag and wrote a twenty‐article statute. Women were banned, so a red‐light district sprang opposite the town. The Qing finally intervened, demanding evacuation.[1s] The miners fled. In January 1886, Chinese troops razed Zheltuga to ash. Survivors scattered, but the legend of the Amur California lives on.
