In 1867, the American barque Rover vanished off Taiwan's southern coast. A violent storm drove the ship onto treacherous coral reefs near Cape Eluanbi. Only a handful survived, only to be captured by the Paiwan tribe. [1s]The tribe mistook the captain’s wife for a man, killing her instantly. [0.8s]Fourteen crew members perished, sparking outrage across the United States. Washington dispatched the USS Ashuelot, demanding justice from the Qing authorities. [1.2s]The punitive expedition failed; marines were struck by heatstroke and gunfire. Eventually a fragile treaty with chief Toketok promised safety for future shipwrecked sailors. [0.5s]The Rover incident foreshadowed larger foreign interventions in Taiwan's history.
