In October 1920, Poland eyed the contested city of Vilnius. General Lucjan Żeligowski led a feigned mutiny, seizing the city. He proclaimed the Republic of Central Lithuania—actually a Polish puppet.[1s] The world believed Lithuania controlled Vilnius, unaware of Poland's scheme. The League of Nations demanded Polish withdrawal, but Warsaw ignored it.[2s] In 1922, a sham election voted to join Poland. [0.5s]Years later, Piłsudski confessed the operation was pre‐planned. This secret coup soured Polish‐Lithuanian ties for decades to come.
