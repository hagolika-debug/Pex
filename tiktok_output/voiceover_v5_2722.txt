On a tiny South Atlantic speck, life was untouched.[1s] A fishing vessel docked, five strangers stepped onto shore.[0.5s] Within days, fever swept through all 284 residents.[0.8s] The first wave peaked in six days, overwhelming the village. Then, as the illness faded, a second surge erupted. Scientists later argued weakened immunity let the same virus strike again.[1s] Ninety‐six percent fell ill; one third battled it twice. The 1971 outbreak became a rare case study of pandemic dynamics. Even isolated corners aren't safe from invisible enemies.[0.5s]
