London, December 1867: A quiet street hides a daring plan. Irish Republicans plotted to free two imprisoned comrades. They buried barrels of gunpowder beneath the prison walls. The explosion erupted at 4pm, shattering nearby homes.[1s] Over 200 people injured, dozens terrified by the blast. The intended tunnel never opened; the plan backfired disastrously.[0.8s] The ‘Clerkenwell Outrage’ shocked Victorian society and the press. Authorities cracked down, arresting several suspects within weeks. The event sparked harsher anti‐Irish laws across Britain.[1.2s] Today, the crater remains a reminder of a forgotten rebellion.
