Imagine rain... [0.8s] but instead of water, chunks of meat descend. It happened on March 3, 1876, in Bath County, Kentucky. A farmer's wife saw red flesh falling from a clear sky.[1s] Neighbors gathered, the smell was like a dead animal carcass. Scientists collected pieces, sending them to labs[0.5s] across the country. The leading theory? Vultures vomiting their meals mid‐flight. In 2004, a preserved sample resurfaced at Transylvania University. Today, the town celebrates with a quirky ‘Meat Shower’ festival.
