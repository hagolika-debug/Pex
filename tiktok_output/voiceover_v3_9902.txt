December 11, 1875, Bremerhaven dock erupted in a deadly blast. The SS Mosel, loaded with a mysterious iron‐cased barrel. Behind it, Canadian fraudster Alexander Keith Jr., posing as 'Thomas'. At 11 AM, the barrel detonated, tearing a four‐meter crater.[1s] Eighty‐one lives lost, over two hundred injured amid screaming crowds. Police uncovered a timed dynamite device designed for an insurance scam. Keith tried suicide, survived briefly,[0.5s] then died confessing his plot. His severed head was displayed in a museum, later lost in war. The Thomas Disaster faded, yet remains a chilling reminder of greed.[2s]
