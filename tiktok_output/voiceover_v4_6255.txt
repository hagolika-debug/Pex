On July 21, 1918, a German U‐boat surprised Cape Cod. U‐156 surfaced three miles off Orleans, guns blazing at unsuspecting ships. The tug Perth Amboy and three barges became the target. Shells missed, some landed on Nauset Beach, shocking sleepy townsfolk. The sound of distant gunfire echoed across the marsh.[1s] Two U.S. seaplanes tried bombing the sub, but their ordnance failed. No one died, yet the town earned a unique distinction.[0.8s] Orleans remains the only contiguous U.S. spot ever shelled by an enemy. A reminder: even quiet shores can hold hidden war stories.
