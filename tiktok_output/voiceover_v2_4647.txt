1932, Australia declared war on an unexpected enemy: the emu. Farmers begged the army to stop emus devouring wheat fields. The government sent Lewis guns and 10,000 rounds of ammunition. Soldiers set up ambushes, but the birds scattered instantly.[1s] Emus sprinted like tanks, dodging bullets and outrunning trucks. The army fired 2,500 rounds, killing maybe fifty birds.[0.8s] The media mocked the 'Emu War', turning soldiers into comedy. Farmers eventually built fences, finally ending the feathered menace. Today, the Emu War reminds us: nature can outsmart militaries.[0.5s]
