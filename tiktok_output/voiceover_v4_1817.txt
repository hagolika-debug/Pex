In 1325, two Italian cities fought over... a legendary bucket. Bologna championed the papal Guelphs, while Modena backed the imperial Ghibellines. Border skirmishes escalated, and Modena seized the Bolognese castle of Monteveglio.[1s] At Zappolino, 7,000 Modenese faced 32,000 Bolognese in a chaotic clash. Legends claim the victors lifted a well‐bucket as spoils, sparking the war's name. Modena's triumph inspired Tassoni's mock‐heroic poem, cementing the bucket in literature.[0.8s] Later, composers turned the tale into operas, keeping the story alive onstage. Today, the original oaken bucket rests in Bologna's civic museum, a quirky relic. A war over a bucket? History proves even the smallest objects can spark epic conflicts.[1.5s]
