In July 1915, a Chicago river turned into a deadly trap. The SS Eastland, meant for a joyful picnic, capsized instantly. Over 2,500 workers and families boarded, unaware of the looming danger. The ship listed, water rushed in, and within minutes, it rolled like a turtle. [1s] More than 800 lives were lost, many children among them. Rescuers scrambled, pulling survivors from the hull while the river churned. The tragedy faded from history, eclipsed by Titanic and Lusitania. [0.5s] Today, a plaque marks the site, reminding us of lives forgotten. [1s] Remember the Eastland, a silent reminder of safety ignored.
