In 1184, a medieval meeting turned into a deadly plunge. Dozens of Holy Roman nobles gathered beneath towering stone arches. The wooden floor, weakened by time, gave way beneath them. They fell into a foul cesspit, drowning in liquid excrement. The stench was lethal.[1s] Nobles gasped for air. Sixty to a hundred aristocrats perished, crushed or suffocated. King Henry VI and Archbishop Conrad clung to iron rails, surviving. The disaster halted peace talks, but the empire’s power shifted. Imagine nobles meeting their doom...[0.5s] all because of a rotten floor.
