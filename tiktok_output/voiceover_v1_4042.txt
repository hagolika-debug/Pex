A tiny strip of land became a free republic by mistake. In 1441, a cartographic error left 330 hectares unclaimed. The locals declared independence, enjoying no taxes or armies. [1s]For centuries they lived without courts or prisons. They turned into Europe's first tobacco haven after the Pope banned smoking. [0.5s]Smugglers, merchants, and debtors flocked to the tax‐free zone. The republic survived wars, because no one cared enough to fight. In 1826, Pope Leo XII ended it; families received a silver coin. Today Cospaia is just a hamlet, but its liberty lives on in festivals.
