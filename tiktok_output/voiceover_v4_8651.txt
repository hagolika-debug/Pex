An American merchant ship vanished off Taiwan’s southern tip in 1867. The barque Rover, laden with tea, set sail from Shantou. On March 12, it struck the coral reef at Qixingyan, sinking fast. Fourteen crew clambered onto a secluded beach, unaware of lurking danger. The indigenous Paiwan tribe, remembering past betrayals, captured and executed them.[1s] The U.S. Consul rushed to China, demanding justice for the dead. Naval forces landed, but heatstroke and guerrilla fire forced a hasty retreat.[0.8s] The tragedy sparked the 1874 Mudan Incident, paving the way for Japanese invasion. Remember the Rover, a forgotten echo of colonial clash and loss.[0.5s]
