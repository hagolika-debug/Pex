In 1932, Australia declared war... on emus.[1s] Veteran farmers begged the government for machine‐gun assistance. The army arrived, armed with Lewis guns, ready to cull. Emus scattered, splitting into tiny, swift squads, evading fire.[0.8s] First volley killed only a few, the birds kept running. Major Meredith called the birds 'tanks'—they seemed unstoppable.[1s] After weeks, 2,500 rounds fired, but less than a hundred birds fell. The media mocked the 'war', soldiers left with bruised egos. Lesson: Even a modern army can be outmaneuvered by flightless giants.
