In 1830, escaped convicts hijacked a ship and sailed east.[1s] They were the brig Cyprus, crew of Australian prisoners. Their desperate quest for water led them to forbidden Japanese shores. Samurai eyes watched the foreign hull, wary of the isolation law. A cannon blast roared, yet the ship stubbornly refused to leave.[0.8s] Negotiations unfolded in sign language, pleading for firewood and water. The convicts' tattoos and strange clothing shocked the local warriors. Finally, after days of tension, the Cyprus slipped away under night.[1.2s] This rare encounter marked the first Australian vessel to touch Japan.
