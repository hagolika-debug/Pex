A forgotten spark that nudged America toward war. In March 1915, the British liner Falaba set sail. German U‐boat U‐28 intercepted her, demanding evacuation.[1s] Among the passengers, American Leon Thrasher faced a fatal fate. The crew gave barely ten minutes before the torpedo struck.[0.5s] Explosion ripped the ship, drowning over a hundred souls.[2s] The US protested, marking its first diplomatic clash with Germany. The incident fueled growing tensions, paving the way to the Lusitania tragedy. Thrasher became the first American casualty of unrestricted submarine warfare. A quiet tragedy that helped shape history's course.
