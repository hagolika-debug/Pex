A forgotten shipwreck sparked an international showdown on Taiwan's cliffs.[1s] On March 12, 1867, the American barque Rover crashed into coral reefs. Fourteen sailors, including Captain Hunt and his wife, washed ashore, vulnerable. Local Paiwan warriors, seeking revenge, slaughtered the survivors without mercy.[0.8s] News reached Washington, igniting calls for a swift American response. The US Navy dispatched a fleet, but dense jungles stalled their advance. The expedition failed, leaving the island's tribes untouched and tensions high. The Rover Incident set the stage for future foreign interventions in Taiwan.[1.2s] Remember this silent tragedy that reshaped East Asian geopolitics forever.
