In the 1880s, miners forged a hidden gold kingdom on the Amur. Gold glimmered along the Albazikha River[0.5s], sparking a frantic rush. Russians, Chinese, even Koreans gathered, creating a melting‐pot frontier town. They elected leaders, raised taxes, and raised a black‐yellow flag. Law was strict.[1s]Violators faced lashes or death. The town boasted hotels, theaters, and a casino called Monte Carlo. Chinese forces demanded evacuation; the settlement burned in 1886. Most fled, but their legend vanished like dust.[0.8s] A forgotten Republic, a wild gold rush echoing through history.
