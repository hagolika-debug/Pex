Imagine a sport where birds sprint home for Olympic glory.[1s] The 1900 Paris Games stretched months, merging a world fair with competitions. Among odd contests, pigeon racing took flight as an unofficial Olympic event. From May to October, 48 French clubs released 7,721 pigeons across six races. Each club sent at least one hundred birds.[1.5s]The sky filled with feathers. No foreign teams entered; the competition remained a pure French showcase. Winners received bronze medals, yet the IOC later erased the event from official records. While racing spared lives,[0.8s]another Olympic event brutally shot hundreds of pigeons. Today, the feathered sprint lives only in obscure history, a quirky Olympic footnote.
