In 1897, a quiet Pennsylvania town became a battlefield. Immigrant coal miners marched, demanding fair wages and safe conditions. They carried flags, hope in every step.[1s] Sheriff James Martin ordered them to halt, tension rose. Shots rang out, nineteen men fell on the dusty road.[0.5s] The echo of gunfire lingered.[2s]The town never forgot. News labeled them 'invaders,' fueling hatred and silence. Decades later, a boulder was erected, honoring sacrifice. Today, the Lattimer Memorial reminds us of forgotten labor struggles. Remember history; silence never erases the truth.
