In summer 1024, a fleet of Viking warriors drifted toward Greece. They were Kievan Rus’, descendants of Scandinavians seeking Byzantine riches. Chrysocheir demanded entry, but Byzantines ordered them to disarm first. He refused, sparking a daring chase through the Dardanelles.[1s] The Rus landed on Lemnos to rest, unaware of the looming trap. Byzantine galleys pretended to negotiate, buying time for a deadly ambush.[0.5s] When the Rus lowered guards on the beach, swords flashed in sunrise. Nearly all eight hundred men were cut down, their names erased by history.[1.2s] Only a handful survived, their story whispered only on Lemnos’ shores. A forgotten clash that shaped medieval power dynamics between Vikings and Byzantines.
