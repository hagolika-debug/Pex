In the 1880s, gold fever birthed a hidden city on the Amur River. Russian and Chinese prospectors settled illegally on Qing territory, naming it Zheltuga. Within months, the town swelled to over 12,000 miners. They forged a flag, [0.5s] a court, and harsh punishments. A casino, theater, and circus entertained the gold‐crazy crowd. The Qing finally intervened, demanding evacuation within eight days. [1s] [2s]Chinese troops returned, razed the settlement, and forced refugees out. By 1886, Zheltuga vanished, its legend living on.
