In 1825, Sunderland's peaceful beach turned into a battlefield. Seafarers protested wage cuts, blocking ships on the River Wear. Local magistrates called in soldiers to force the strikers back. Thousands gathered, women and children watching, tension mounting.[1s] Soldiers opened fire, bullets ripping through the crowd. At least seven dead, dozens wounded, lives shattered instantly. [2s]The massacre was buried, forgotten for two centuries. 2025 bicentennial commemorations finally bring the tragedy back. Remember forgotten injustices; history's echoes demand justice today.[1s]
