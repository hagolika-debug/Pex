In 1916, a Spanish liner vanished on a moonlit night.[1s] The Príncipe de Asturias set sail from Barcelona, bound for Buenos Aires. Carnival celebrations roared aboard, while a hidden storm brewed offshore. At dawn, towering waves slammed the hull,[0.5s] tearing a massive breach. The ship listed, steam exploded, and within minutes, it sank. Only one lifeboat launched, leaving dozens to drown in darkness. Survivor Gregorio Siles clung to a crate,[0.8s] drifting for days. He later whispered rumors of secret gold hidden in the hold. Official records list 445 dead, 143 rescued, many unnamed passengers. Centuries later, the tragedy remains a forgotten chapter of maritime history.
