In 1900, Colombia’s bloodiest conflict erupted in the mountains. The Thousand Days War pitted Liberals against Conservatives for control. Deep in Santander, the Palonegro ridge became a deadly trench line. On May 11, both armies clashed, guns echoing for fifteen brutal days.[1s] Soldiers suffered malaria, heat, and constant rain, turning war into nightmare. Over 2,500 lives were lost, many buried in unmarked graves.[0.8s] The defeat forced Liberals to retreat, ending hopes for immediate victory. Palonegro remains a forgotten scar, teaching the cost of endless war.[2s] Remember Palonegro, the silent echo of Colombia’s hidden history.
