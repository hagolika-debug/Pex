In 1914, Oregon's governor declared martial law on Copperfield. Saloon owners ran nightly gambling halls, defying prohibition laws. Governor West ordered his secretary Fern Hobbs to shut them down. On a cold January train, Hobbs arrived with five armed militiamen.[1s] The townspeople greeted them with bunting, unaware of impending crackdown. Hobbs read the governor's proclamation, ordering all saloons closed instantly.[0.5s] Militiamen stationed at each doorway, seizing liquor, weapons, and gambling tables. Saloon owners sued, but the court upheld West's martial law authority. By 1915 the town burned, its saloons never reopened.[1s]
