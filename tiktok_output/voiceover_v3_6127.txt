July 26, 1184, Erfurt's grand hall brims with noble power. King Henry VI presides, seeking to settle a bitter territorial feud. Above ancient cesspits, rotting wooden beams hide a deadly secret. Nobles crowd the room, armor clanking, voices echoing.[1s] Suddenly, the floor shatters, sending nobles plummeting into darkness. They fall into a filthy latrine, water boiling with waste.[0.8s] Dozens drown in excrement, their cries muffled by stench. King Henry survives, perched on a stone alcove above the horror. The disaster reshapes imperial politics, leaving power vacuums across the empire. A grim reminder: even the mighty can fall through...[2s]...their own folly.
