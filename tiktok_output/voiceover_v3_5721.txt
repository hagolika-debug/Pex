In 1832, 56 settlers declared independence between America and Canada. They formed a constitution, naming their nation Indian Stream. [0.8s] The tiny republic boasted a council, militia, and tax system. But both U.S. and British officials claimed the land as theirs. A 1834 debt dispute sparked arrests, escalating tensions dramatically. [1s] The New Hampshire sheriff threatened militia invasion. By August 1835, Indian Stream voted to rejoin New Hampshire. [0.5s] Yet Canada protested, sending diplomats and troops. The 1842 Webster‐Ashburton Treaty finally sealed the border, erasing the republic.
