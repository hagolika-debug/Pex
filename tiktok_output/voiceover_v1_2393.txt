In 897 Rome, [0.5s] a dead pope faced judgment. Stephen VI ordered Formosus' corpse exhumed from its tomb. The lifeless body was propped on a papal throne. He was accused of perjury, illegal papacy, and simony. A deacon answered for the dead, while the crowd gasped. Guilty![0.5s] Stephen sliced off the pope's blessing fingers. Stripped, weighted, Formosus' corpse was cast into the Tiber. [1s]The river carried his body; rumors claimed miracles returned. Later synods nullified the trial, banning future corpse courts forever.
