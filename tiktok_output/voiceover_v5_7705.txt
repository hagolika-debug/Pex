A stray dog sparked an international crisis in 1925.[1s] Greek and Bulgarian rivalry over Macedonia had been simmering for years. A Greek soldier chased his dog across the border, prompting a deadly shot. Bulgarian border guards fired, killing a Greek captain and a soldier. Greece issued a 48‐hour ultimatum, then invaded Petrich with troops.[0.5s] The League of Nations demanded an immediate ceasefire, ending the clash.[1s] Bulgaria secured a diplomatic win; Greece paid £45,000 in compensation. The ten‐day 'War of the Stray Dog' exposed early limits of global peacekeeping. Today, the incident reminds us how tiny sparks can ignite wars.
