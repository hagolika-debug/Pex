A train full of picnickers turned into a battlefield on New Year's Day.[1s] Remote Broken Hill, Australia, 1915: mining town surrounded by endless red desert. Two men on a camel‐driven ice‐cream cart[0.5s] raised rifles. Bullets ripped through open ore trucks, shattering laughter into screams.[1s] Passengers fled, some thinking it was a salute, others praying. Armed police and soldiers rushed to the scene, guns blazing.[0.8s] The gunmen fled to Cable Hill, only to be surrounded.[1.2s] Four civilians died, seven wounded, the attackers killed, Australia shocked. The only WWI battle on Australian soil, forgotten yet haunting history.
