What if Germany once ruled part of South America? In 1528, Emperor Charles V sold Venezuela to the Welser bankers.[0.5s] They founded Neu‐Augsburg, now called Coro, as their Caribbean capital. Ambrosius Ehinger led daring expeditions, seeking the fabled El Dorado.[1s] In 1529 he founded Maracaibo, naming it New Nuremberg. But disease, hunger, and fierce indigenous resistance decimated the German settlers.[0.8s] By 1546 the Spanish reclaimed the colony and executed the last Welser leaders. The Welser charter was revoked, ending Germany's brief American empire. Today, the story lives only in forgotten archives and old maps.
