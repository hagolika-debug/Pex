August 19, 1871, Newton's Hide Park turned into a killing field.[1s] It all began with a bitter political argument between two lawmen. Billy Bailey and Mike McCluskie fought, shots rang, Bailey fell dead. A week later, McCluskie returned to gamble, unaware of revenge brewing. Three friends of Bailey stormed the dance hall, guns drawn, tension soaring. Hugh Anderson taunted McCluskie, shot his neck, chaos erupted instantly.[0.8s] A sick teenager, James Riley, rose, emptying his rifle on the attackers. Seven men fell, Riley walked out, vanished forever into legend.[1.2s] The Newton Massacre left thirteen dead, a forgotten story eclipsed by O.K. Corral.
