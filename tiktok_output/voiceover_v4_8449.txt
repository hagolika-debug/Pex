In April 1945, a secret mission saved priceless horses. The Nazis hid the elite Lipizzaner stallions in Hostau. Soviet troops planned to kill them for meat.[1s]The world gasped. General Patton authorized a daring cavalry raid to rescue them. American soldiers and German Wehrmacht fought side‐by‐side against the SS. They herded over 300 pregnant mares and foals into waiting trucks. Just as a Soviet tank appeared, the convoy sped toward freedom.[0.5s] Today, those horses perform in Vienna, a living reminder of bravery.
