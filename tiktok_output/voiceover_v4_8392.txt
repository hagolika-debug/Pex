In 1184, a royal summit turned into a nightmarish plunge. Nobles from across the Holy Roman Empire gathered beneath Erfurt’s ancient cathedral. King Henry VI called the Hoftag to settle a bitter land dispute. The creaking wooden floor groaned under their weight, then gave way suddenly.[1s] They fell into the church’s hidden cesspit, a pit of foul waste. Dozens drowned in sewage, others crushed, some suffocated by the stench.[0.8s] Miraculously, King Henry and Archbishop Conrad clung to iron rails, surviving.
