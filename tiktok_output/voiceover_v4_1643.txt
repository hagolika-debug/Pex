Imagine a tiny country hidden between New Hampshire and Canada.[0.5s] Born in 1832, it declared independence to escape double taxation. The 1783 Treaty of Paris left the border ambiguous, sparking the dispute. Locals drafted a constitution, naming it the Republic of Indian Stream.[0.8s] Three hundred residents elected officials, even a justice of the peace. Both US and British sheriffs demanded taxes, leading to armed standoffs. A hardware debt triggered a shootout.[1s]The militia nearly invaded. By August 1835, New Hampshire militia forced annexation; the republic dissolved. Today, the town of Pittsburg marks the spot of this vanished nation.
