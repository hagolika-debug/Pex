London, 1814: A brewery's massive vat suddenly exploded, spilling beer. The wooden tank held over 300,000 gallons of porter.[0.5s] When the vat burst, a 15‐foot wave of suds flooded streets. Neighbors in the St Giles slum watched in horror as beer crashed. Eight people died—some at a wake, others swept by foam. [2s]The flood turned a bustling market into a slippery, tragic river. Spectators paid to see the ruined brewery, turning tragedy into spectacle. [1s]The disaster forced breweries to replace wooden vats with safer concrete. Today, the site houses the Dominion Theatre, but the beer tsunami lives on.
