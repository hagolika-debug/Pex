In 1900, quiet Konitz became a flashpoint of hysteria. A missing student sparked rumors of ritual murder.[1s] Local newspapers blamed Jews, inflaming townspeople beyond reason. Police found the body in frozen lake ice, but fear prevailed. Trials exposed fabricated evidence, yet mobs demanded vengeance. The emperor ordered justice, but anti‐Semitic sentiment lingered.[0.8s] Two journalists were convicted of libel, a rare victory. Years later, the case faded, leaving scars on the community. Remember Konitz,[0.5s] a warning of fear turned into tragedy.
