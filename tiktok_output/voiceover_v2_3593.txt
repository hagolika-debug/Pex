In December 1871, four Ryukyuan ships were torn off course by a typhoon. One vessel vanished, while another crashed onto Taiwan's rugged western shore.[0.5s] Sixty‐nine passengers clung to hope, but three perished trying to reach land. The remaining 66 trekked inland, meeting two Chinese men who warned them. Ignoring warnings, they entered Paiwan territory, where hospitality turned into tragedy.[1s] On December 18, the Paiwan ambushed them, killing fifty‐four sailors mercilessly. Nine survivors hid with a Hakka trader, evading death for forty days. Finally rescued, they returned home, sparking Japan's 1874 Taiwan invasion.[0.8s]
