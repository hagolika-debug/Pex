On a quiet March morning, Kentucky sky began to rain... Red chunks fell, startling the farmhand, who thought it was...[1s]...blood. Witnesses described a snapping sound as the pieces thudded onto dirt. Scientists from Louisville rushed to analyze the odd meat, publishing frantic reports. Early theories ranged from cursed rain to alien debris,[0.5s] but none convinced. The most plausible explanation? Vultures vomited their meals mid‐flight.[0.8s]...Scientists agree. Samples sent to New York revealed tissue resembling mammalian lung and muscle. Even today, a preserved piece sits in a Kentucky museum, sealed in alcohol. A strange rain that still haunts locals, reminding us how weird nature can be.
