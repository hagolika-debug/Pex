In 1814, London witnessed a disaster no one could imagine.[1s] At the Horse Shoe Brewery, a massive wooden vat suddenly burst. A torrent of dark porter surged[0.7s], flooding streets like a liquid tsunami. Eight innocent souls drowned, including children attending a wake. Crowds gathered, some scooping beer, turning tragedy into chaotic revelry. The brewery survived financially only after the government refunded lost taxes. This catastrophe forced the industry to replace wooden vats with steel. Today, the site hosts the Dominion Theatre, erasing the memory of beer. A forgotten flood reminds us how even ordinary places can unleash chaos.[1s]
