In the Caribbean, a tiny island sparked American liberty. St. Eustatius, Dutch colony, housed a thriving Jewish merchant community.[0.5s] They secretly funneled gunpowder, muskets, and supplies to Washington. British Admiral Rodney called the island a ‘nest of vipers.’[1s] The cargo traveled in hidden barrels, evading Dutch embargoes. By 1778, thousands of barrels supplied the Continental Army. When Britain seized the island in 1781, they looted everything. The community was expelled, its synagogue left silent forever.[0.8s] Their hidden aid helped win independence, yet history barely remembers them.
