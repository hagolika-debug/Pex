In 897, Rome staged the strangest courtroom drama ever.[0.5s] Pope Stephen VI exhumed his dead predecessor, Pope Formosus. The corpse sat on a throne, robed like a living pontiff.[1s] A deacon was forced to speak for the silent dead pope. Stephen accused Formosus of perjury, illegal papacy, and lying. The dead pope was found guilty, his fingers cut off. His body, stripped, was tossed into the Tiber River. [2s]The crowd revolted, demanding justice for the murdered pope. Soon after, the Synod was annulled, restoring Formosus's legacy.
