Imagine a wild gold rush, but on the Chinese border. In 1883, miners founded Zheltuga, the 'California on the Amur'. Russians and Chinese built a bustling town of 12,000 prospectors. They raised their own flag, black and yellow, symbolizing soil and gold.[0.8s] Law, taxes, even a hospital existed—mirroring a real republic. The empire watched.[1s] Qing China angry at illegal gold. In 1885 Chinese forces demanded evacuation; miners ignored the warning. When soldiers returned,[0.5s] they razed the town, erasing its gold. By 1886 Zheltuga vanished, leaving legends of a lost republic. History forgot, but its story shines like buried treasure.
