In 1584, Spain launched a daring colony at the Strait of Magellan. They named it Rey Don Felipe, dreaming of a new empire. Harsh winds, barren soil, and endless winter crushed their hopes. Within months, disease and starvation left only thirty survivors.[2s] English explorer Thomas Cavendish arrived in 1587, finding a ghost town. He renamed the ruin Port Famine.[1s] a warning for future adventurers. Archaeologists in 2022 uncovered the original foundation coin, confirming the location.[0.5s] The site now stands as a stark reminder of colonial hubris. History repeats: ambition unchecked meets nature's unforgiving truth.
