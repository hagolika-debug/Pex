January 1917, a silent New Jersey town became a fireworks nightmare. The Canadian Car & Foundry factory churned millions of shells for the war. A spark ignited at a workbench, flames licked the ceiling.[1s] Within minutes, half a million shells detonated, lighting the sky like daytime. New Yorkers watched in horror as the blast illuminated the Hudson horizon. Switchboard operator Tessie McNamara shouted, 'Get out or go up!'[0.8s] Miraculously, no one died, thanks to her frantic warnings. Decades later, Germany paid millions in reparations for the silent disaster. Today, a modest park honors Tessie, the unsung heroine of Kingsland.
