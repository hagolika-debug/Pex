In Brazil's south, a 19th‐century cult sparked a deadly war. Led by prophet Jacobina Mentz, the Muckers believed they were chosen. Their followers held secret meetings in the muddy valleys, fearing persecution.[0.5s] When authorities tried to arrest them, the Muckers fought back fiercely. A bloody showdown erupted on July 19, 1874, consuming the Maurer homestead.[1s] Hundreds perished, including children, as flames devoured their final refuge. The government declared victory, yet whispers of Mucker survival lingered. Today, the tragedy remains a forgotten chapter of Brazil’s turbulent past. Remember the Muckers—forgotten rebels whose story still echoes in silence.[2s]
