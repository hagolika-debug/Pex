In 1932, Australia declared war on a flock of emus.[0.5s] Farmers begged the government for help as birds devoured crops. The army arrived with machine guns, thinking it would be easy. Emus scattered, outrunning bullets and turning the battlefield into chaos.[1s] Soldiers mounted guns on trucks, only to watch them jam. After days of futile firing, only a few birds fell. The media mocked the ‘Emu War,’ and the army withdrew.[0.8s] Farmers kept fighting the birds with fences, not rifles. Today, the battle lives on as a quirky lesson in hubris. The emus won, proving nature can outsmart soldiers.
