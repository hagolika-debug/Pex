November 1758, fog blankets Pennsylvania frontier, soldiers brace for night battle. General Forbes orders a pursuit of French raiders near Loyalhanna Creek. Two Virginia regiments approach, mistaking each other for enemies in the darkness. Gunfire erupts, killing officers and sowing chaos among the startled troops.[0.5s] George Washington rides into the blaze, sword raised to stop the carnage.[1s] He slashes rifle muzzles, forcing soldiers to lower their fire. The fog lifts, revealing the tragic mistake and dozens of fallen comrades. Washington survives, forever marked by the night his life hung by a thread.[0.8s] History barely remembers this skirmish, yet it shaped his resolve for future wars.
