A forgotten Mexican outpost turned into a nightmare kingdom.[1s] Clipperton Island: a barren coral atoll, 1,080 km from Mexico. In 1906, Mexico sent families to claim the remote atoll. The Mexican Revolution cut supply ships, leaving colonists starving. Scurvy and death claimed most men; only women and children survived. Lighthouse keeper Victoriano Álvarez seized guns and declared himself king. He ruled with a rifle, raping and murdering any dissent.[0.8s] Two brave women struck back, killing the tyrant with a hammer. A U.S. Navy gunboat arrived hours later, rescuing the eleven survivors.[1s] Clipperton remains uninhabited, a haunting reminder of forgotten suffering.
