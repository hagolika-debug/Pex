In 1840, Oregon pioneers faced a cattle shortage that crippled survival. They hatched a daring plan: build a ship, sail to California. On Swan Island, settlers hammered timber, forging the Star of Oregon. Hudson's Bay Company blocked supplies, forcing secret trades with neighboring settlers. Enter Captain Charles Wilkes, who supplied papers and rigging, unlocking the voyage. The ship launched amid fog, creaking timbers echoing hope.[1s] The journey began. Reaching San Francisco, they traded the vessel for three hundred fifty cattle. Over seventy days, they herded livestock across rugged terrain back to Oregon. Their daring gamble birthed Oregon's independence.[0.8s] A forgotten triumph. Imagine building a ship to feed a frontier. Would you dare?
