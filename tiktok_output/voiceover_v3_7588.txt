1835. Ohio and Michigan teetered on the brink of war.[1s] A 468‐square‐mile strip called the Toledo Strip sparked the clash. An 1800s map misplaced Lake Michigan, shifting the imagined border. Ohio claimed the land, promising a new county and court. Michigan’s 'Boy Governor' raised a militia, ready to defend. Only one bloodied incident: a penknife wound at Philips Corners.[0.5s] President Jackson sided with Ohio, denying Michigan statehood. Michigan traded the strip for the Upper Peninsula, a 'barren wasteland'. Today, the war lives only in quirky trivia and a tiny statue.[1s]
