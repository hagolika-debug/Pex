What if America began under a Swedish flag?[1s] In 1638, Swedish settlers anchored on the Delaware River. Peter Minuit, former Dutch governor, plotted the secret colony. They erected Fort Christina, the region’s first permanent European outpost. Those Swedes introduced the log cabin, America’s iconic pioneer home. In 1655, Dutch Admiral Stuyvesant forced the Swedes to surrender.[2s] Today, Swedish place names and churches linger across Pennsylvania. Old Swedes’ Church still hosts worship, unchanged since 1698. A hidden chapter that shaped America, still waiting to be discovered.[1.5s]
