In 1894, Assam endured a massacre the world forgot. British rule imposed sky‐high land taxes, crushing peasant livelihoods. On January 28, villagers marched, demanding relief from the 70% hike. Authorities dismissed their pleas, ordering a brutal lathi charge. Soldiers opened fire, blood spraying across the crowd.[1s] Silence fell. Officially 15 died, but eyewitnesses claim over a hundred. The tragedy earned the name Assam's Jallianwala Bagh. Today, a marble column marks the site on Farmer Martyrs Day. Remembering the silenced peasants reminds us: history repeats when ignored.[0.8s]
