In 1910, six friends plotted a daring naval deception. They disguised themselves as Ethiopian royalty, complete with beards and turbans. Virginia Woolf, then unknown, joined the troupe in full brownface.[0.8s] The group sent a telegram, claiming the Emperor would inspect HMS Dreadnought. On a foggy February morning, they boarded the battleship, greeted by bewildered officers.[1s] The Navy, assuming a royal visit, offered a full salute and luncheon. When asked to fire the guns, the hoaxers politely declined.[0.5s] Parliament debated the incident, questioning naval security and absurdity. The prank made headlines worldwide, proving that a clever ruse can outsmart a battleship.
