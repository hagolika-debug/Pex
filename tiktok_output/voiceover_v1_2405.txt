In 1876, a daring scheme unfolded to steal a president. Chicago counterfeiters, desperate and bold, hatched the audacious plan. Their target: Abraham Lincoln’s embalmed body, still lying in Springfield. They demanded two hundred thousand dollars ransom—[2s]—to release the corpse. The Secret Service, still unassigned to protect presidents, went undercover. At midnight, agents waited inside Oak Ridge Cemetery, guns hidden. The thieves arrived, but police arrested them before any theft. Lincoln’s remains were moved, encased in steel, buried deep. A bizarre chapter of history, forgotten by most, remains eerie.[1s]
