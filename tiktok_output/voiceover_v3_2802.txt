Rome, 897 AD: factions fight, a dead pope awaits judgment. Pope Stephen VI orders Formosus’s corpse exhumed from the catacombs. The body is propped on a throne, silent witnesses stare. Stephen accuses the dead of perjury, illegal papacy, and treason. The corpse is found guilty.[1s] The crowd erupts in disbelief. Three fingers of the right hand are severed, symbols shattered. Formosus’s body is stripped, dressed as a layman, then thrown into the Tiber.[0.5s] His rotting form drifts ashore, rumors claim miracles still occur.[1.2s] Months later, the synod revokes the trial, restoring Formosus’s honor.
