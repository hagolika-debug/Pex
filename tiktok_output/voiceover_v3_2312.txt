In 1944, a quiet Illinois town faced an unseen terror. Residents awoke to a sweet, choking odor drifting through their homes. Within minutes, they felt limbs go numb, coughing, vomiting. [2s]Panic spread like wildfire across Mattoon. Police received over two dozen frantic calls in just two weeks. [1s]Witnesses described a tall figure in a dark cap, clutching a strange spray gun. Some claimed the culprit was a woman disguised as a man. Scientists later argued mass hysteria, industrial fumes, or a real attacker? [1.5s]The mystery vanished as quickly as it began, leaving only whispers.
