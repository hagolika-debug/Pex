In 1184, nobles met for peace—then fell into a cesspit. They gathered inside Erfurt's ancient church, under fragile wooden floors.[0.8s] The floor gave way under dozens of heavy aristocrats. They plunged into a foul latrine, drowning in medieval sewage. Sixty to a hundred nobles perished, crushed, drowned, or asphyxiated.[1s] King Henry and Archbishop Conrad clung to iron rails, living. The tragedy halted peace talks, but Henry's rise continued. History remembers the ‘Latrine Disaster’ as a macabre reminder. What if the empire’s fate had been sealed beneath excrement?[1.5s]
