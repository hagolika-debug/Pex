In 1855, the tranquil Paraná River turned deadly. USS Water Witch, a modest American survey ship, drifted northward. Paraguayan cannons roared.[1s]The river erupted in fire. One American sailor fell, his blood staining the water. Paraguayan troops suffered losses too, caught in crossfire. Negotiations stalled.[0.8s]Diplomacy fizzled like damp gunpowder. President López demanded reparations, the US demanded apology. A show of force followed in 1859, forcing Paraguayan concession. The incident sparked a brief naval showdown, now lost to history.[1.2s]
