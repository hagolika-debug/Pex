In 1873, Indiana towns battled for the county seat. Centerville defended its jail, while Richmond plotted to move it. A mob of fifty armed men surrounded the sheriff’s residence at night. Gunfire shattered windows,[0.5s] filling walls with bullet holes. The guards held out, but a cannon was aimed at the door. They loaded scrap metal, fired, and the iron door exploded. The attackers rushed in, guards surrendered, and peace was promised. A posse of a hundred men arrived by train at dawn.[1s]The battle ended. Centerville lost the seat; Richmond became the new county hub.
