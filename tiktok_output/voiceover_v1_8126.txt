In 1832, a tiny borderland declared its own nation. The land lay between New Hampshire and British Canada, disputed for years. Treaty of Paris left the exact border ambiguous, sparking endless arguments. [2s] Residents faced double taxes, sheriffs from both sides demanding payment. They formed the Indian Stream Republic, drafting a constitution on July nine. [1s] The fledgling state elected a council, with Luther Parker as justice. In 1835 New Hampshire militia threatened invasion, forcing annexation talks. By August 5, 1835, Indian Stream surrendered, becoming part of New Hampshire. [0.8s] Today, the story lives only in a historic marker and forgotten maps.
