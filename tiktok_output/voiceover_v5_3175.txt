In 1810, tiny Banda Islands sparked a revolt over nutmeg. The Dutch VOC monopolized nutmeg,[0.8s] crushing local growers' freedoms. But war in Europe weakened the Dutch, opening cracks. British ships appeared.[1s] The isolated garrison trembled under pressure. Asian soldiers and locals allied, targeting Dutch forts and warehouses. They rose.[0.5s] The island ignited into chaotic battle. British forces landed, joining mutineers to oust the Dutch. For months Banda slipped from Dutch control, trading flourished. When the Napoleonic wars ended, the islands returned to Dutch rule.
