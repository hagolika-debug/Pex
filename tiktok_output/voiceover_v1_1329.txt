Rome, 897 AD:[1s] a dead pope rises from his tomb. Stephen VI orders the corpse to be exhumed, propped on a throne.[0.5s] The dead Formosus faces accusations of perjury and illegal papacy. A deacon answers for him,[1s] echoing through the silent basilica. The judges strip his papal robes, exposing the rotting corpse. They sever his blessing fingers, symbolically ending his authority. The verdict: guilty. Formosus’s acts declared null and void. Crowds roar,[0.5s] the body cast into the Tiber, drifting away. History remembers a trial of a corpse, a chilling reminder.
