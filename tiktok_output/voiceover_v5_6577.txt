In 1914, a tiny border clash sparked a forgotten war. Deep in Angola's savanna, Portuguese forts watched the horizon.[0.8s] A German medical team crossed into neutral Angola, seeking supplies. They met Portuguese patrol; mistrust simmered under scorching sun. When ordered to disarm, commander Schultze-Jena drew his pistol.[1s] Shots rang; five Germans fell, one captive taken.[1.5s] The bloodshed ignited German retaliation, leading to the Battle of Naulila. World War I's African front rarely taught, yet it shaped colonial borders. A forgotten flashpoint, echoing the costs of empire.
