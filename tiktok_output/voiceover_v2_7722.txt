In November 1943, a massive troopship disappeared without a trace. The HMT Rohna carried nearly two thousand U.S. soldiers across the Mediterranean. A German He‐177 bomber released a radio‐guided glide bomb onto its side. [1s]The explosion ripped a massive hole, flames devouring decks in seconds. Overloaded lifeboats jammed; soldiers clung to rusted rails, desperate for escape. [0.5s]Rescue ships arrived, pulling hundreds from icy water, but many perished. The Allies classified the disaster, silencing families and the press for years. Only in the 1990s did declassified files finally reveal the tragedy. Today, the Rohna’s story reminds us how history can be hidden.
