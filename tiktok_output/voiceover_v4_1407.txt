In 1325, two Italian city‐states clashed over pride. Bologna, the Guelphs, defended the Pope; Modena backed the Emperor. A petty dispute over a well’s wooden bucket[0.5s] sparked legend. But true cause: centuries‐long border tension and castle theft. On November, 32,000 Bolognese marched to Zappolino. Modena’s 7,000 defenders struck first, surprising the massive army. Bologna fled, leaving behind a humble bucket as trophy.[1s] The battle claimed roughly 2,500 lives, yet the bucket endured. Today, Modena still displays the bucket, a reminder of absurd war.[0.8s]
