In Brazil’s southern frontier, a mystical uprising erupted in 1873. A German healer named Jacobina Mentz claimed to be a living prophet. Her followers became the 'Muckers', while skeptics were called 'Spotters'. They set fire to rival farms.[0.5s]The night glowed red. Brazilian troops marched, crushing the sect with brutal force. The final siege began.[1s]The house ignited, screams silenced. Jacobina escaped briefly, only to be captured and brutally executed. Their story faded, yet echoes remain in Brazilian folklore today.
