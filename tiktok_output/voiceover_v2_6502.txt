Deep in Siberia, miners built a secret gold republic. Between 1883 and 1886, it thrived like California. Russians and Chinese lived side by side, speaking a mixed pidgin.[0.5s] No women, no laws—only a black‐and‐yellow flag. They taxed gold, built hospitals, even a casino.[0.8s] The Qing Empire finally noticed the rogue settlement.[1s] In January 1886, Chinese soldiers razed Zheltuga to ashes. The gold vanished, but the legend of the 'Amur California' lives on.
