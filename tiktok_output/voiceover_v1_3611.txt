Rome, 897 AD: political chaos, rival factions battling for power. Pope Stephen VI orders a shocking decree—dig up his predecessor. The dead Pope Formosus rises from his tomb.[1s] Stripped of robes, his corpse sits on a throne, silent witness. Stephen accuses him of treason,[0.8s] perjury, illegal bishops. A deacon answers for the dead, pleading with trembling lips. Judgment:[0.5s] three fingers cut off, papal authority revoked. Body cast into the Tiber, floating like a cursed relic. Public outrage erupts; Stephen is strangled, the Synod erased.
