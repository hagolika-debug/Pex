In 1610, a massive Portuguese carrack faced Japanese samurai. The ship, Nossa Senhora da Graça, carried silk and silver treasures. A dispute in Macau sparked a deadly feud between cultures.[1s] Japanese samurai launched a four‐day siege on the anchored vessel. Captain André Pessoa ignited the gunpowder store, choosing death over surrender. Explosions ripped the hull, sending glittering silver into the bay.[0.8s] The battle terrified Japan, reshaping foreign trade policies forever. Decades later, treasure hunters still search the wreck’s hidden riches. A forgotten clash that forged a new era of isolation.[0.5s]
