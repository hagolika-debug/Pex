May 22, 1915: Britain’s deadliest rail crash unfolds. Two signalmen, a sleepy shift change, a fatal oversight. A troop train barreling forward, unaware of a stationary local. [1s]The first collision erupts, metal screaming against steel. A second express slams into the wreckage, magnifying chaos. [0.8s]Pintsch gas ignites, turning wood to raging inferno. Over 200 soldiers perish; many bodies never recovered. Signalmen convicted, yet both returned to rail work after prison. Centennial remembrance: plaques, services, and a haunting melody.
