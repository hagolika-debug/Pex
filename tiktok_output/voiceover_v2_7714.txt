July 10, 1902, Johnstown's Rolling Mill Mine erupts in tragedy. A scorching summer sweltered, miners toiled beneath the relentless sun. Deep shafts breathed methane, the invisible killer waiting for a spark. At eleven o'clock, a lantern flickered, igniting a deadly flame. The blast silenced the tunnels, afterdamp choking every breath.[2s] Rescuers rushed in, fans roaring, hoping to force fresh air. Only eighteen miners emerged alive; one hundred twelve lost forever. The city's steel mills kept humming, forgetting the miners' sacrifice. Today, the disaster rests in dusty archives, unseen by most.[1s] Remember the hidden heroes, their story echoing through forgotten coal dust.[0.5s]
