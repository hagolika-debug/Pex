Imagine a flood... not of water, but of beer. On October 17th, 1814, London faced an unprecedented catastrophe.[0.5s] A massive wooden vat burst, releasing a tidal wave of porter. The foam surged through narrow streets, crushing everything.[1s] Eight innocent souls drowned, some at a wake turned tragedy. Spectators paid to watch the beer torrent, turning disaster into spectacle. Meux Brewery nearly went bankrupt, saved only by a tax rebate. The tragedy forced breweries worldwide to abandon wooden vats forever. A beer tsunami that still bubbles in history's forgotten corners.[2s]
