In 1741, a tiny Indian port defied a mighty Dutch empire. King Marthanda Varma united fragmented kingdoms, craving pepper profits. The Dutch blockaded Colachel, cutting trade lifelines with iron will.[0.5s] Fishermen turned guerrillas, sinking ships under moonlit waves. A fake cannon line fooled the Dutch navy.[1s] Then a single cannonball ignited the stockade's powder stores. Chaos erupted, Dutch troops fled in panic.[0.8s] Captured commander De Lannoy became Travancore's admiral, reshaping its army. The victory halted Dutch dominance, a forgotten triumph echoing today.
