Imagine a gold rush kingdom hidden on the Chinese border. In 1883, miners struck gold along the Amur River's banks. They declared the Zheltuga Republic, a rogue 'California on the Amur'. At its peak, 12,000 hopeful prospectors from Russia, China, and beyond gathered. They built hotels, theaters, even a casino called Monte Carlo of the Far East. A strict code punished thieves with 500 lashes[1s]; chaos barely survived. The Qing dynasty finally noticed, demanding evacuation within eight days. In early 1886, Chinese troops razed the town[0.8s]; the golden dream vanished. Today, the ruins whisper of a vanished multinational gold city. Did you know this forgotten republic?
