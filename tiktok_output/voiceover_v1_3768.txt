A hidden WWI battle unfolded on America's quiet Cape Cod shores. July 21, 1918:[0.5s] German submarine U‐156 prowled the Atlantic waters. The U‐boat surfaced three miles offshore[0.8s] and unleashed its deck guns. It struck the tug Perth Amboy, sinking a schooner and three barges. Shells rained onto Nauset Beach,[1s] the only US soil ever shelled. Coast Guard surfboat braved the barrage, rescuing thirty‐two trapped sailors. Two Curtiss seaplanes dropped bombs that failed to detonate. The attack ended after an hour, leaving no casualties on land. Today a modest monument marks Orleans' brief encounter with war.
