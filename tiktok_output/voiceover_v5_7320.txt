In 1932, Australia declared war on... emus.[1s] Farmers begged the government as wheat fields vanished. Three soldiers armed with machine guns marched to Campion. First shots fired; the birds scattered like living ghosts.[0.5s] Bullets jammed, emus outran trucks, laughing at metal. After days, only thirty birds fell; the war failed.[1s] The media mocked the ‘victors’—the emus outsmarted soldiers. Government later gave farmers bounties, not bullets. The Great Emu War lives on as a bizarre lesson.
