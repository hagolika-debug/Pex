Rio de Janeiro, 1910. A modern navy hides a brutal secret.[0.5s] White officers punished black sailors with whips, despite a national ban. The final straw: a sailor receives 250 lashes for two bottles of cachaça.[1s] Night of November 22. Enlisted men seize the dreadnought Minas Geraes. They fire warning shots, demanding the end of the lash.[0.8s] President Fonseca weighs brutal suppression against granting amnesty.[0.5s] Congress passes amnesty; rebel ships are ordered to stand down. Most mutineers are discharged, many sent to the Amazon jungle.[1.2s] The revolt reshapes Brazil’s navy, ending corporal punishment forever.
