December 1900, a remote Scottish lighthouse stands against relentless storms. Three keepers—Ducat, Marshall, McArthur—maintain the light each night. On December 15, a fierce gale rattles the western landing. When the supply tender arrives weeks later, the doors are sealed. Inside, a clean lamp, a set table, and a lonely canary. The men vanished without a trace.[1s]The sea claimed them? Investigators concluded a sudden wave swept them away.[0.8s]But doubts linger. Legends speak of ghostly lights and spectral footsteps on the cliffs. Over a century later, the mystery still haunts Scotland's rugged coast. Will the truth ever surface? [1.5s] Only the waves know.
