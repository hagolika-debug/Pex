In 1832, a tiny nation rose between the US and Canada. The land was called Indian Stream, nestled among four pristine lakes. A blurry 1783 treaty left the border undefined, sparking conflict.[0.8s] Both British and American tax collectors demanded money, double‐taxing the settlers. Fed up, locals drafted a constitution and declared independence on July 9. They elected Luther Parker as justice, forming a modest government.[1s] But New Hampshire’s sheriff raised militia, demanding the territory’s surrender. After tense negotiations, the republic folded on August 5, 1835.[0.5s] The dispute finally ended with the 1842 Webster‐Ashburton Treaty, sealing borders.
