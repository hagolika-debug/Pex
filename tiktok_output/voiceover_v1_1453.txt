In 1518, Strasbourg turned into a massive, uncontrollable dance floor.[1s] Hundreds swayed for days, believing it was a divine curse. Physicians[0.5s] prescribed more dancing, thinking exhaustion would stop the mania. The mayor hired musicians, hoping rhythm would calm the frenzy. Some danced to death[1s]; over thirty collapsed, never to rise again. Modern scholars argue mass hysteria, ergot poisoning, or social stress triggered it. The town finally banned music, forcing the dancers to stop. Today, the event remains a baffling mystery, inspiring art and research.
