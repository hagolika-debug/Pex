London 1814, a quiet brewery turned deadly nightmare. A massive wooden vat burst[0.5s], unleashing hundreds of thousands of gallons. The porter wave crashed into St Giles, a cramped slum. Eight souls perished, some at a funeral wake. [2s]Neighbors fled as beer flooded streets, turning cobbles into rivers. [1s]Spectators paid to watch the wreckage, a morbid spectacle. The disaster forced breweries to replace wooden vats with steel. Today, the site houses the Dominion Theatre, masking the tragedy. A forgotten flood, but its splash still echoes in history.
