On a moonlit night, 100,000 Austrians marched toward Karánsebes. Scouts met Romanians offering schnapps, sparking a drunken dispute among troops. An angry shout echoed.[1s]Then a single shot rang out. Confusion erupted as hussars and infantry fired upon each other, believing enemies nearby. Orders shouted 'Halt! Halt!' were misheard as 'Allah! Allah!'.[0.8s] Emperor Joseph II entered the chaos, but his pleas fell on deaf ears. The Ottomans arrived two days later, discovering a shattered Austrian camp. History calls it the worst friendly‐fire disaster, a tragic military blunder. Even the mightiest forces crumble when communication fails and panic spreads.
