In July 1918, a German U‐boat surfaced off Cape Cod. The United States had just entered World War I, barely a year earlier. U‐156, the Kaiser’s largest submarine, opened fire on a tugboat. The steam tug Perth Amboy and four barges became a floating target.[1s] Patrol planes scrambled; Ensign Lingard’s seaplane raced across choppy waters. A single 260‐pound bomb hung beneath the wing, trembling. The bomber dropped the payload, shells exploding around the doomed tug. The attack marked the only enemy fire on contiguous United States soil.[0.8s] History forgot the battle, but its echoes linger on Cape Cod.
