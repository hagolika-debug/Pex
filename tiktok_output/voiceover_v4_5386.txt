In 1830, a rag‐tag crew of convicts set sail. They hijacked the brig Cyprus in Hobart, escaping harsh penal colonies. After months drifting, they limped into Japan's forbidden waters at Mugi Cove. Samurai disguised as fishermen approached, wary of foreign stench.[1s] Cannon fire crackled, yet the brig stubbornly refused to move.[0.5s] The convicts begged for water and firewood, signing with trembling hands. Strange gifts—boomerangs, tattoos, a pet dog—confused the samurai. With a sudden wind, the Cyprus slipped away, vanishing into night.[1s] Centuries later, scholars finally proved the first Australian ship reached Japan.
