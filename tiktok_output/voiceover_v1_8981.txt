In 1915, a German lieutenant crossed into Maine with dynamite. His mission: cripple the Vanceboro rail bridge feeding Allied troops. Winter night, frostbitten hands, he lit the fuse.[1s] The bridge shuddered. Explosive blast cracked iron, yet no train was derailed. Local townsfolk awoke to shattered windows and confused whispers. Police arrested Horn, but jurisdiction tangled between US and Canada.[0.5s] He claimed he wanted only to stop supplies, not kill. Sentenced to 30 days, then 18 months in Atlanta federal prison. After release, extradition sent him to Canada, where he vanished.[1.2s] A forgotten sabotage, echoing the hidden wars of espionage.
