In 1902, a German ship sparked a deadly Haitian showdown. The Markomannia, a bustling Hamburg liner, neared Cap‐Haïtien. Rebel gunboat Crête‐à‐Pierrot boarded, demanding hidden weapons. Captain Nansen protested, but the rebels seized the cargo. Germany dispatched SMS Panther. [1s] Cannons roared across the harbor. Admiral Killick detonated Crête‐à‐Pierrot's magazine, [0.5s] sealing his fate. The U.S. watched warily, fearing a breach of the Monroe Doctrine. Germany claimed victory.
