In the wild Siberian frontier, a gold haven emerged. Between Russia and China, miners built a bustling town. They called it Zheltuga, the 'Golden River'.[1s] At its peak, 12,000 hopefuls lived there. Law was iron—stealing meant flogging, murder meant death.[0.8s] Russian and Chinese forces eventually converged, ending its brief glory. The town burned, its gold buried beneath ash and myth. Today, only whispers remain of the Zheltuga Republic. A lost chapter of frontier lawlessness, forever hidden.[0.5s]
