On a cold February night, the sky ignited with fire. A procession of forty fireballs stretched across the horizon, silent and eerie. They lingered, each burning for over thirty seconds, lasting five minutes total.[1s] Some listeners swear they felt the ground tremble, hearing thunder-like roars.[0.5s] Witnesses from Canada to Brazil reported the same celestial parade, all night. No radiant point appeared; astronomers later called it the 'Cyrillid Shower'. Some proposed a temporary satellite, others imagined a broken lunar volcano.[0.8s] The event sparked worldwide awe, yet faded into forgotten history. Look up tonight; perhaps the sky still holds secret fireworks.
