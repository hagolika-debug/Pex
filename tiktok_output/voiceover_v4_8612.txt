February 1942, Los Angeles erupted in an impossible night sky. A weather balloon drifted high, sparking a citywide panic.[0.5s] Anti‐aircraft cannons roared, lighting the night like volcanic fire. Shells exploded everywhere.[1s] Civilians fled in terrified chaos. Five innocent lives lost—three car crashes, two heart attacks. The military blamed a false alarm, calling it 'war nerves'. Media sensationalized phantom planes, fueling UFO myths for decades. The sky finally cleared.[2s] Los Angeles returned to uneasy normalcy. Today, a quiet museum commemorates the night the city fought ghosts. A reminder: fear can turn shadows into wars.
