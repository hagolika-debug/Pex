When New Hampshire militia approached, the assembly surrendered after a tense standoff.[1s] A minor hardware-store debt sparked a bizarre cross‐border chase and gunfight.[1.5s] By August 1835, Indian Stream was annexed to New Hampshire, ending its sovereignty.
