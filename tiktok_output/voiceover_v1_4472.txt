In 1910, Portuguese Macau faced a sudden pirate uprising. Pirates seized seventeen schoolchildren, demanding a thirty‐five thousand dollar ransom. Governor Eduardo Marques declared martial law over Taipa and Coloane. First Portuguese landing was ambushed; a soldier fell dead. Reinforcements arrived aboard the gunship Macau, shells screaming across the sea. [1s]The gunship Pátria and cruiser Dom Amélia unleashed relentless bombardments. Hundreds of Chinese villagers perished, while pirates fled into a storm. [0.8s]Portugal annexed Coloane, solidifying its Macau enclave forever. A stone monument now stands, whispering the forgotten battle.
