July 21, 1918: a German U‐boat opened fire on Cape Cod. The tug Perth Amboy and four coal barges became the unsuspecting targets. Shells thundered across the water, missing the ships entirely.[1s] The beach erupted. Vacationers gathered, phones raised, watching history unfold on the sand. Only two crew members were seriously wounded, their names later lost to time. A boy waved the American flag, daring the enemy with a .22 rifle.[0.8s] Courage echoed. The submarine aimed to cut a transatlantic telegraph cable, not just sink barges. Months later, U‐156 vanished in the North Sea, its fate still a mystery. The Battle of the Barges remains America’s only WWI combat on home soil.[0.5s] Remember.
