On New Year's Day 1915, a hidden WWI battle erupted in Australia. A mining town's picnic train became the target of a daring ambush. Two Afghan cameleers, Gool Mohammed and Mullah Abdullah, sought revenge for war. Armed with homemade bandoliers, they opened fire at ten past ten. Only ten passengers were hit, but three lives were tragically lost.[2s] The locals rushed to battle, seizing rifles from a nearby gun club. Police and army clashed with the attackers on a rocky outcrop.[1s] After the shootout, the attackers were killed and the town mourned. This forgotten skirmish sparked Australian enlistment, echoing through the Great War.
