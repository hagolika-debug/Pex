August 11, 1880, a sunny beach trip promised joy. Over a thousand hopeful passengers boarded the new West Jersey Line. Two trains split, racing against rain and a single track. The first train slipped onto a siding, unaware of the following. [2s]Suddenly, the second locomotive slammed into the rear, steam exploding. Passengers were thrown, some leapt into the icy river below. Eighty lives lost, the town's population swelled with tragedy. [1s]The disaster vanished from headlines, buried in forgotten archives. Today, a historic marker reminds us of Mays Landing’s silent tragedy.
