In 1910, Brazil's navy faced a shocking rebellion. Sailors, fed up with brutal whippings, plotted their revenge. On November 22, mutineers seized the dreadnought Minas Geraes. They captured three more warships, turning firepower against the capital. Rio's skyline trembled as the rebels aimed at the harbor.[1s] Government cannons fired, but their crews hesitated.[0.5s] The mutineers held firm. Congress negotiated peace, promising to end the lash forever. The amnesty saved the fleet, yet many sailors vanished into the Amazon. Today, João Cândido remains a forgotten hero of freedom.
