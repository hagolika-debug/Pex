October 3, 1780, North Carolina: a quiet farm hides a secret war. Fourteen militia men prepared to face a British foraging party of hundreds. A boy spots the troops, whispers warning to Captain Thompson.[1s]Tension rises. Patriots conceal behind trees, waiting for the perfect moment to strike. A stray foot knocks over a beehive.[0.8s]Angry bees swarm the battlefield. Gunfire erupts; buzzing insects create chaos, confusing the British soldiers. Believing they face a larger force, the Redcoats retreat in panic. The farm’s wagons stall, horses fall; supplies abandoned for good.[1.2s] The ‘Battle of the Bees’ proves even nature can turn the tide of war.
