On a crisp September night, a paddle steamer left Kent. Hundreds of working‐class families boarded for a cheap day out. Near Woolwich, the Princess Alice collided with the massive Bywell Castle. Panic erupted as water flooded decks, heavy dresses dragging victims down.[1s] Rescue ropes were thrown, but the polluted Thames claimed many lives. Estimates say six hundred souls vanished, some drifting as far as Erith. [0.5s]The disaster sparked stricter safety laws and the first public swimming pools. A forgotten mass grave now rests in Woolwich Cemetery, marked only by a modest stone. [1s]Today, the Thames still hides the echo of those lost, a silent reminder.
