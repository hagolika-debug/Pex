In 1887, Ohio farmers fought a hidden war over water. The Six Mile Reservoir, a stagnant 2,000‐acre mosquito swamp. Residents blamed the reservoir for relentless malaria and ruined crops. They tried cutting the dike at night, but cold halted them. Governor Foraker sent militia, yet the rebels vanished like ghosts. On April 25, 200–400 men stormed the dam, dynamite roaring. Only one fatality occurred: a young guard accidentally shot himself. The reservoir was crippled forever.[1s]Nature reclaimed the cursed water. Today, a marker stands.[0.8s]Reminding us of the 'No Compromise!' fight.
