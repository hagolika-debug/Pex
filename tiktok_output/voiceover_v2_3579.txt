In the 1880s, a hidden gold city rose on the Amur. Nestled in Siberian wilderness, Russian and Chinese miners forged a lawless republic. At its peak, twelve thousand prospectors lived like a frontier Las Vegas. They elected a president, collected taxes, and even built a hospital. Violators faced whips, death, or exile.[1s]Justice was brutal. A casino called Monte Carlo drew gamblers losing thousands of rubles nightly.
