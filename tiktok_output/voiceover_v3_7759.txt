February 1903, Stanaford, West Virginia, became a hidden battlefield. The clash erupted from the New River coal strike, demanding union rights. A posse of five hundred armed men marched on the mountain town. Miners holed up in a schoolhouse, firing back at the invaders. Bullets ripped the night sky.[1s]The hills echoed with relentless fire. Three miners died in their beds, bodies drenched in blood. Mother Jones arrived,[0.5s] finding shattered homes and children wailing for fathers. The state never marked the site;[0.8s] today, only forest remembers. Remember Stanaford, a forgotten fight for dignity and justice.
