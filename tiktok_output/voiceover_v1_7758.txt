1878, a night on the Thames turned into a nightmare. The paddle steamer Princess Alice left Sheerness, full of hopeful workers. At 19:20, she collided with the massive Bywell Castle, sparks flying. The hull cracked, water surged in.[1s] Panic spread like wildfire. Over 600 souls vanished, many pulled from toxic, sewage‐filled water. The disaster sparked stricter safety laws and the rise of public swimming pools. Yet today, the Princess Alice tragedy remains largely forgotten in history. A solemn memorial now stands in Woolwich Cemetery, honoring the lost. Remember the silent victims,[0.8s] lest their story drown again.
