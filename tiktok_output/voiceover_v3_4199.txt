In the 1880s, a gold rush birthed a rogue republic. Nestled on the Amur River, miners built a lawless city. At its peak, twelve thousand prospectors from five continents converged. They raised a black‐and‐yellow flag, declaring independence from Qing China.[1s] Gold flowed, casinos thrived, even a circus performed nightly. Violence erupted; lashings and whips kept order among the miners. But in 1885, Chinese troops arrived, demanding evacuation.[0.5s] The settlement burned, disappearing like a mirage of fortune.[2s] Today, Zheltuga’s ghost remains a forgotten chapter of frontier history.
