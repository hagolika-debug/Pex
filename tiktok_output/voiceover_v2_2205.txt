New York City, 1906: a hidden health crisis erupts. Doctors forced tonsil removals on elementary students without parental consent. Children returned home bleeding, mothers screamed in terror.[1s] Rumors spread: surgeons were stealing organs for profit. On June 27, angry mothers stormed school doors, demanding answers.[0.5s] Police clashed with crowds; chaos turned streets red with blood. The riot forced the Board of Health to halt mandatory surgeries. Legacy: a century later, the event fuels vaccine skepticism.[0.8s] Remember the tonsil riots: a warning about medical consent.
