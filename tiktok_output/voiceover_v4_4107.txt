In 1830, a ragtag crew of convicts crashed into Japan's isolation. They escaped Tasmania aboard the brig Cyprus, hijacking their captors. Samurai artists recorded a foreign ship anchored at Mugi Cove. The convicts begged for water, firewood, and a chance to repair. Japanese commanders ordered cannon fire, but the ship lingered. The brig endured relentless bombardment.[1.5s] Still, it did not surrender. After days, a secret sign‐language pact let them catch the wind. They sailed away, becoming the first Australian vessel to set foot in Japan. A forgotten tale resurfaced in 2017.[0.8s] Proving history's hidden chapters.
