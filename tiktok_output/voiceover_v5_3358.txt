In 1939, Odisha’s tribal peasants gathered to protest oppressive taxes. Led by war veteran Nirmal Munda, they demanded land rights and fairness. British officials arrived, promising arrest. Tension rose.[1s]Shots rang out instantly. Soldiers opened fire on the unarmed crowd, killing dozens on the field. Bodies were hidden in limekilns, and many were buried without ceremony. Gandhi learned of the massacre; his heart ached in silence.[2s] News reached Nehru, who planned a satyagraha that never materialized. Today, the site sits forgotten, a ghost of colonial cruelty. Remember the Amko Simko victims; their story deserves a louder voice.[0.8s]
