In 1919, a tiny Washington town erupted into chaos. It was Armistice Day, a celebration turned deadly. Veterans from the American Legion marched past the IWW union hall. Tensions boiled; gunfire erupted, echoing through the crowded streets.[0.5s] Five Legionnaires fell, and dozens were wounded in an instant.[1s] The IWW members were arrested, tried, and sentenced to decades. Public outcry faded; the tragedy became a footnote in history.[0.8s] Centennial events in 2019 finally revived memory of the lost. Now, a monument stands, reminding us of labor's hidden scars.
