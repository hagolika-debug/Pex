April 25, 1939, a quiet tribal village in Odisha gathered. They convened to defend land rights, led by veteran Nirmal Munda. British officers stormed the assembly, demanding Munda's immediate surrender. The crowd stood firm, refusing to betray their leader.[0.5s] A fallen cap sparked panic, misread as an attack. Without warning, police unleashed indiscriminate fire on the unarmed gathering.[1s] Bullets tore through families, killing at least forty‐nine brave souls. Bodies were dumped into the nearby quarry, silence swallowing truth.[0.8s] Decades later, the Amko Simko tragedy remains hidden, a forgotten scar.
