In 1741, a tiny Tamil port changed colonial history forever. The Dutch East India Company craved pepper, sending warships to enforce monopoly.[0.5s] King Marthanda Varma of Travancore refused to bow, rallying his people. Local Mukkuvar fishermen turned tides, sabotaging Dutch boats under moonlit night.[1s] They mounted palm trunks on carts, fooling enemies into seeing fake artillery. A single Travancore cannonball hit a powder barrel, exploding the Dutch stockade.[0.8s] Out of ammo and food, the Dutch surrendered within days, retreating forever. Captured commander De Lannoy became Travancore’s admiral, reshaping its army. The victory halted Dutch power in India, a forgotten triumph. History missed this story—now you know the battle that reshaped empires.
