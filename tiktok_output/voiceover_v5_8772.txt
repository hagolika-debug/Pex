In 1900, Michigan’s quiet lakes hid a dark secret The Burt Lake Band lived peacefully on their ancestral shoreline A greedy land developer coveted their land for profit Sheriff Ming and deputies arrived, armed with a writ They forced families out, then set their homes ablaze.[1s] Nineteen families watched their lives turn to ash Only the stone church survived, standing as a lone witness.[0.8s] The Burn‐Out sparked legal battles that lasted decades Today, descendants fight for recognition and justice
