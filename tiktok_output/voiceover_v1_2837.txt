In 1873, a remote Brazilian valley erupted with a deadly religious uprising. German immigrants, led by prophet Jacobina Mentz, called themselves the Muckers. She claimed visions, healing powers, and warned of an imminent apocalypse. Neighbors labeled them heretics,[0.8s] sparking violent clashes between Muckers and the Spotters. Muckers burned homes, killing families, while the army prepared a brutal retaliation. July 19, 1874: soldiers stormed Jacobina’s homestead, flames engulfing the night.[1.5s] Dozens perished; Jacobina escaped briefly before being captured and brutally executed. The massacre ended the revolt, but legends of the Muckers haunt the region still. A forgotten war of faith and fear, echoing across Brazil’s forgotten past.[0.5s]
