In 1918, a quiet Texas village faced a brutal tragedy. Rangers, backed by cavalry, accused locals of aiding cross‐border bandits. January 28th, they rounded up fifteen men and boys. The men were taken to a hill, never to return.[1s] Rangers opened fire, echoing across the barren desert.[0.5s] Fifteen lives extinguished, families left grieving in silence.[0.8s] The massacre stayed hidden, only whispered in local folklore. Centuries later, archaeologists uncovered bullets confirming the horror. Remember Porvenir, a scar on history demanding acknowledgment.
