In 1184, a deadly meeting turned into a bathroom nightmare. King Henry VI summoned dozens of aristocrats to Erfurt for peace talks. They gathered in a centuries‐old church perched atop a rotten floor. Suddenly, the wooden floor gave way, yawning beneath their feet.[1s] Nobles plunged into a foul cesspit, screams echoing through stone. Sixty to a hundred aristocrats drowned, crushed, or suffocated by stench. Only King Henry and Archbishop Conrad clung to iron rails, surviving. The dispute was forgotten, but the tragedy haunted medieval Europe.[0.8s] Today, the Erfurt Latrine Disaster reminds us power can sink...[0.5s]into filth.
