In 1863, a bizarre battle raged over bird eggs. The Farallon Islands, towering cliffs, hosted the nation’s largest seabird colony. Two rival egging companies fought for rights to the prized murre eggs. Pacific Egg Company claimed exclusive ownership, but rivals refused to back down. When David Batchelder’s crew landed, armed men opened fire.[1s] Gunshots echoed over the sea;[1s] two men died in the clash. The conflict ended with the Egg Company gaining sole control. Yet by 1881, the government finally evicted all eggers.[0.5s]
