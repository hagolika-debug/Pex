Imagine a country born from a simple map mistake. In 1441, two surveyors missed a strip of land. 350 farmers declared independence, claiming no taxes, no rulers. No army, no courts—disputes settled in neighboring towns. Tobacco flourished; the tiny republic became Europe’s first duty‐free hub.[0.5s] 1642 the Pope threatened excommunication, yet Cospaia smoked freely.[1s] For 385 years the borderless haven survived, untouched by war. 1826 the Pope sealed its fate; residents received a silver coin.[0.8s] Today Cospaia is a hamlet, but its flag still flies.
