In 1867, an American merchant ship vanished off Taiwan's coast.[0.8s] The barque Rover struck a coral reef near Cape Eluanbi, sinking fast.[0.5s] Fourteen crew members scrambled ashore, only to be seized by Paiwan warriors. Among them, Captain Hunt's wife Mercy was mistaken for a man, then killed. The tribe sought vengeance for earlier foreign killings of their own people. U.S. Consul Charles Le Gendre raced to Beijing, demanding Chinese intervention. A punitive expedition landed in June, only to falter under scorching heat.[1s] The failed raid led to the 1874 Mudan Incident, sparking Japanese invasion. Today, the Rover tragedy remains a forgotten flashpoint of Taiwan‐U.S. history.[0.6s]
