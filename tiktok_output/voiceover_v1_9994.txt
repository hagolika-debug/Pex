In 1814, London faced a disaster unlike any other. A massive wooden vat at Meux's Horse Shoe Brewery suddenly burst. Over 300,000 gallons of porter crashed through the brewery walls. A 15‐foot beer wave flooded the narrow streets of St Giles. Eight innocent people drowned, including children at a wake.[1s] Crowds gathered, treating the tragedy as a bizarre spectacle. Meux's brewery survived, but wooden vats vanished forever.[0.5s] The flood sparked modern safety standards for industrial containers. Today, the site hosts the historic Dominion Theatre. Imagine a tsunami of beer—London's forgotten flood.[2s]
