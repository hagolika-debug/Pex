Imagine a council of nobles, suddenly falling into a cesspit.[1s] July 1184, Erfurt's Petersberg church, ancient wooden floor trembling beneath them. They convened to resolve a fierce land feud between two powerful lords. The floor collapsed, plunging the assembly into a foul, overflowing latrine. Sixty to a hundred nobles drowned, were crushed, or asphyxiated by the stench.[0.8s] Only a handful survived, including future Emperor Henry VI, clinging to iron rails.[0.5s] The original dispute faded, but the horror lingered in medieval chronicles. Centuries later, the Erfurt latrine disaster still haunts history lovers worldwide.
