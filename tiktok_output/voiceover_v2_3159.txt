In Brazil's misty south, German settlers birthed a deadly cult. Jacobina Mentz, a mystic woman, claimed prophetic visions. Her followers, the Muckers, gathered, chanting, healing, and planning revolt. Tensions rose as locals labeled them heretics, sparking violent clashes. May 1874: Jacobina preached apocalypse,[1s] ordering the extermination of sixteen families. June's bloodbath: homes torched, families slaughtered, terror spreading across valleys. July 19, 1874 – troops stormed their compound,[1s] flames devouring lives. Jacobina fled briefly, then captured, brutally killed with her child. The Muckers vanished, yet whispers linger in Brazil's forgotten history. A hidden war reminding us: faith can spark both hope and horror.[0.8s]
