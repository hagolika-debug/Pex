In 1915, Taiwan erupted under Japanese rule with a hidden rebellion. Han farmers and indigenous Taivoan people united against colonial oppression. It began at the Xilai Temple, where mystic Yu Qingfang preached revolt. They stormed police stations, disguised as postmen delivering mail.[1s]Chaos erupted. But one station resisted; guards stayed shut, foiling the surprise attack. Yu's forces burned the Nanzhuang station, igniting fear across the valley. Soon, betrayal struck.[0.8s]Yu was captured at a banquet. Japanese troops responded with brutal reprisals, massacring villages and beheading rebels. The Tapani Incident faded from history, yet its echo still haunts Taiwan.
