On April 24, 1865, the steamboat Sultana left Vicksburg. Designed for 376 souls, she crammed nearly 2,400 Union soldiers aboard. Rushed repairs left a weak boiler seam, a ticking time bomb. At 2 a.m. on April 27, the boilers exploded violently.[0.5s] The river turned red with fire and floating bodies.[1s] Over 1,800 men perished, eclipsing the Titanic in sheer loss. Just days earlier, Lincoln’s assassin was captured, stealing headlines. The tragedy faded fast, forgotten by history’s textbooks.[0.8s] Today, divers still retrieve relics, honoring the lost Sultana souls.
