In 1814, London faced a disaster nobody expected. A massive wooden vat at Meux's Horse Shoe Brewery suddenly burst. Over 300,000 gallons of porter roared out like a liquid tsunami. The frothy wave smashed into the cramped St Giles slums,[1s] flooding streets. Eight innocent souls drowned, including children at a mournful wake. Spectators gathered, watching the beer cascade,[0.8s] some even tasting the flood. The tragedy forced brewers to abandon wooden vats forever. Today, the site is the Dominion Theatre, a reminder of chaos. History's oddest flood still bubbles up[1.2s] in forgotten stories.
