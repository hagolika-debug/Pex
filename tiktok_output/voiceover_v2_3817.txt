In 1915, a Chicago river turned into a deadly playground. The SS Eastland, a gleaming excursion steamer, promised a sunny picnic. Overloaded with lifeboats after Titanic, the ship became a ticking time bomb.[1s] 2,500 families crowded the deck, unaware danger lurked beneath. At 7:28 a.m., the vessel tipped, listing like a falling tower.[1.2s] Water rushed in, passengers flung like rag dolls, screams echoing everywhere. Neighbors tossed planks, children clutched floating crates, hope flickered briefly. Over 800 lives vanished, most under 25, the city wept.[1s] Forgotten today, the Eastland remains a haunting reminder of hubris.
