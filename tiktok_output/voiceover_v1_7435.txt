In 1900 a small Prussian town erupted into blood‐soaked hysteria. Student Ernst Winter vanished after dinner his body found dismembered weeks later. Antisemites seized the tragedy branding it a ritual murder by Jews. The town's whispers turned into riots torches lighting the night.[1s] Military forces intervened yet Jews faced harsher sentences than their attackers. Two journalists were convicted of libel but later won seats in Reichstag. Truth emerged slowly revealing jealousy as the real motive.[0.8s] The case cleared the accused yet the Jewish community never fully recovered. The Konitz Affair reminds us how fear can distort justice forever.
