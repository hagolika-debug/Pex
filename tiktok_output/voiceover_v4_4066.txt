Winter 1855, Devon awoke to impossible hoofprints spanning miles. Fresh snowfall revealed single‐file tracks, each four inches long. Marks stretched from Exmouth to Teignmouth, crossing rooftops and rivers. Footprints ignored walls, haystacks, even narrow drain pipes. Locals whispered of the Devil, sermons echoed the mystery.[1s] Scientists suggested donkeys, kangaroos, even balloon ropes as culprits.
