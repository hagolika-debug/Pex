On March 3, 1876, sky rained something bizarre. Allen Crouch’s wife was scrubbing soap when it began. Red flakes fell, landing on grass like strange snow. Neighbors gathered, some even dared to taste the mystery. Scientists examined samples; they identified lung tissue from livestock. The leading theory? Vultures vomited their meals mid‐air. A startled flock expelled food, creating a flesh rain. Imagine looking up, seeing nothing, then...[1s] meat drenching you. The incident made headlines, baffling newspapers across the nation. A true reminder: nature can be stranger than fiction.[0.8s]
