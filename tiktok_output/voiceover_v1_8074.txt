In 1272, a simple theft sparked a medieval war. A peasant stole a cow at a jousting tournament.[0.5s] The bailiff executed him, igniting fury among his lord. Lord Guy of Namur rallied troops, demanding revenge. [2s]The conflict erupted across Condroz, burning villages. Hundreds died in the Battle of Ciney, blood soaked fields. [1s]King Philip III of France forced a truce. In 1278, the status quo returned, the cow reclaimed? A quirky war remembered only in dusty chronicles today.
