Imagine a hidden gold rush kingdom rising on the Amur. In 1883, miners forged the Zheltuga Republic, a lawless frontier.[0.5s] At its peak, twelve thousand prospectors from twelve nations called it home. They elected presidents, raised a black‐yellow flag, and even built a hospital. Their laws were harsh.[1s]Disobedience meant lashes or death. Women were banned, yet a secretive prostitution trade flourished nearby. In 1886, Qing troops stormed the settlement, burning it to ashes.[1s] The ghost town vanished, but legends of the Amur’s ‘California’ linger. Would you strike gold in a lawless republic?
