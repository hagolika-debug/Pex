Imagine a country that existed because of a map mistake. In 1441, a strip of land fell between Florence and the Papal State. No treaty mentioned[0.5s] it, so the locals declared themselves free. They lived without taxes, armies, or prisons for nearly four centuries. [1s]Their economy thrived on smuggling[0.5s] salt and tobacco. When the Pope banned tobacco, Cospaia became the only legal grower. [0.8s]The tiny republic even minted its own silver coin in 1826. On June 26, 1826, fourteen families signed the act of surrender. The land split between Tuscany and the Papal State, ending its freedom. Today, the vanished republic lives on in a tiny hamlet and stories.
