In 1883, gold fever birthed a hidden frontier on the Amur. Russian and Chinese miners built a bustling town called Zheltuga. They forged a crude law code, punishing thieves with brutal whips.[1s] Gold flowed, spawning casinos, hotels, and even a miniature orchestra. Women were barred, yet a shadow market thrived across the river. Cixi demanded evacuation; Chinese troops marched, silence fell over the camp.[2s] In January 1886, soldiers torched Zheltuga, erasing its golden dream. Today, only ruins whisper the tale of the 'California on the Amur'.
