In 1754, a sleepy Connecticut town heard a terrifying roar. Residents feared a French attack, grabbing muskets in the dark. The source? Thousands of bullfrogs battling in a dried mill pond. [1s]The croaking was so loud, it shook the village. Colonel Dyer led a scouting party to investigate the mysterious noise. They found dead frogs, a chaotic battlefield of amphibians.[0.8s] The incident became a legend, inspiring ballads and even an opera. [0.5s]Today, Windham honors the battle with a frog‐covered bridge. A bizarre war with no blood, yet forever etched in history.
