In 897, Rome erupted over a dead pope's trial. Pope Stephen VI seized power amid endless papal turnover. He ordered Formosus' corpse exhumed and dragged to the altar. The rotting body sat on a throne as a deacon spoke.[0.8s] Accused of perjury, illegal papacy, and wielding multiple bishoprics. The dead pope was declared guilty.[1s]His fingers were severed. Stripped of vestments, his body was tossed into the Tiber.[0.5s] Romans rioted, Stephen was captured and strangled in his cell. Future synods erased the verdict, banning trials of corpses forever.
