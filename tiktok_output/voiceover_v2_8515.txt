July 21, 1918, a quiet Cape Cod morning turned chaotic. German U‐boat U‐156 surfaced, guns blazing, targeting a humble tugboat. Shells ripped the sky; sand erupted like fireworks over Nauset Beach. [2s]The town watched, stunned, as foreign fire struck American soil. Captain Tapley abandoned ship, his crew clung to lifeboats amidst chaos. [1s]Coast Guard surfmen scrambled, launching a small boat into gunfire. A daring seaplane rose, attempted to bomb the submarine—its bomb dud. [0.5s]Bombs failed, the U‐boat retreated, leaving only smoke and stories. No lives were lost, yet Orleans remembered America’s only WWI mainland attack.
