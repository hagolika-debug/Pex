In 1908 Oklahoma, towns fought over a simple piece of paper. Being the county seat meant courts, taxes, and endless prosperity. Checotah celebrated a telegram announcing temporary seat status. Eufaula’s attorney rallied voters, flipping the vote by a single point.[1s] Armed men boarded a train, heading south to seize the records. Gunfire erupted at Main Street; two men fell instantly.[0.8s] Former marshal Johnson persuaded Jones to lay down arms. Eufaula won the final vote, cementing its county seat forever. The bloodied battle faded, but history still whispers its tale.[1s]
