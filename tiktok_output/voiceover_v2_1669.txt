On August 3, 1825, Sunderland's North Sands turned deadly in a shocking Seafarers protested wage cuts, blocking ships, demanding fair pay Authorities sent twelve soldiers from the 3rd Light Dragoons to intervene Over a hundred men, women, and children gathered on the beach Soldiers opened fire,[1s] killing at least seven and wounding many The screams echoed across the dunes in darkness.[2s]The beach fell silent Coroner deemed the deaths justifiable homicide, sparking public outrage Two centuries later, a ceremony honors the forgotten victims.[0.5s] Remember the North Sands massacre, a silent chapter of labor history
