In July 1184, nobles gathered in Erfurt for a tense summit. King Henry VI called the Hoftag, hoping to settle a bitter feud. Hundreds of heavy robes and armor crowded the ancient wooden floor. [0.5s] Suddenly, the floor gave way, plunging nobles into darkness below. They fell into a foul cesspit, drowning in rotting sewage. Some were crushed by falling beams; others suffocated from the stench. [2s] The disaster claimed up to a hundred aristocrats, shocking the empire. Miraculously, King Henry and Archbishop Conrad clung to iron rails, surviving. [1s] This macabre tragedy reminded even the powerful that fate can crumble beneath them.
