In 1754, sleepy Windham, Connecticut awoke to an impossible roar. Colonists feared a French attack, so they grabbed muskets at dawn.[0.5s] The source? Thousands of bullfrogs battling each other in the drying pond. Their croaks rose like drums, shaking windows and rattling nerves.[1s] Scouts rode out, lanterns trembling, only to find a sea of dead frogs. The town laughed, calling it the 'Battle of the Frogs'—a lasting legend. Soon, ballads spread, and the frogs became Windham's quirky mascot. Even presidents saw the statues, and the town built a Frog Bridge. A bizarre war of croaks, remembered forever in stone and song.[0.8s]
