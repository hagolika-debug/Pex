On a tiny Tamil coast, a kingdom faced a Dutch empire. King Marthanda Varma dreamed of a spice‐rich, independent realm. The Dutch East India Company blockaded Colachel, demanding tribute. Local Mukkuvar fishermen turned the sea into a deadly trap.[1s] They forged fake cannons from palm trunks, fooling enemy gunners. A single cannonball struck a powder barrel, igniting chaos.[1s] Dutch supplies exploded, leaving troops defenseless on the beach. Marthanda Varma captured 24 officers, offering them a new allegiance. The defeat ended Dutch ambitions in India, reshaping colonial power.
