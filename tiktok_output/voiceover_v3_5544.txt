In 1932, Western Australia declared war on a flock of runaway emus. Farmers begged the government after crops were destroyed by the giant birds. The military sent soldiers, two Lewis guns, and plenty of ammunition.[0.8s] But the emus scattered, outrunning bullets and even the machine‐gun fire. [1s]The soldiers withdrew, exhausted, and the birds kept marching. Prime Minister James Scullin ordered the operation to stop after costly failures. The emus won, costing the army twelve men and over a thousand rounds. [0.5s]Today, the Emu War remains a quirky reminder of nature’s stubbornness. History laughs, but those birds wrote a strange chapter in military lore.
