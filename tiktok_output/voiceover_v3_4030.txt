A mutinous brig, the Cyprus, sailed from Tasmania toward destiny. Convicts seized the ship, fleeing punishment,[0.5s] chasing freedom across oceans. They reached Shikoku's Mugi Cove, greeted by wary samurai scouts. Samurai painted a water‐colour, capturing the foreign vessel's eerie silhouette. The crew begged for water and firewood,[1s] their pleas met with suspicion. Cannon fire roared; the ship shuddered, yet the convicts held fast. Negotiations via sign language bought three days to repair the sails. When winds finally rose, the Cyprus slipped away,[1.2s] leaving legend behind. Today, this hidden encounter proves Australia's first ship touched Japan.
