In 1830, Australian convicts hijacked the brig Cyprus. They fled Tasmania, sailing across oceans toward an unknown destiny. After months at sea, they sighted Japan's forbidden shores.[1s] Samurai spies disguised as fishermen approached the strange, stinking vessel. The crew begged for water and firewood, pleading in sign language. [2s]The samurai warned: leave now, or face cannon fire. Bullets slammed the hull, shaking the brig as the wind refused. [1s]Miraculously, the ship slipped away, disappearing into twilight. Historians now confirm the Cyprus was the first Australian ship to reach Japan.
