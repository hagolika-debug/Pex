Deep in the Amur River, a secret gold kingdom rose In 1883, miners from Russia and China converged, chasing glittering promises They called it 'California on the Amur'.[1s] A lawless city blossomed At its peak, twelve thousand prospectors lived in tents, taverns, theaters Women were banned, yet a hidden red-light district thrived across the river The settlers forged their own flag: black and yellow, gold's colors But Qing officials finally noticed.[0.8s] They sent troops to crush the dream In January 1886, Chinese soldiers burned the town, erasing its memory Today, only whispers remain of this forgotten republic, hidden in history
