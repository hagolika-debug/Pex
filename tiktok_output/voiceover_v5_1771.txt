On July 21, 1918, German U‐156 opened fire on Cape Cod. A tugboat and its barges became the unexpected targets of war. Residents rushed to the dunes, eyes fixed on exploding craters.[1s] The nearby Chatham Naval Air Station scrambled a Curtiss HS1L flying boat. Pilot Ensign Lingard struggled to lift off with a heavy bomb aboard.[0.5s] The U‐boat's 15‐centimeter deck guns rattled the sea, missing the seaplane. A bomb finally dropped, splash only, no explosion.[0.8s] U‐156 dove beneath the waves, ending the only WWI attack on the U.S. mainland. Today, Nauset Beach’s quiet dunes hide a forgotten chapter of wartime history.
