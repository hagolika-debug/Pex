In 1862, a lone British trader met a fatal fate. He rode the Tokaido road near Kawasaki, ignoring warnings.[0.5s] A samurai procession thundered past, their swords gleaming in sunlight. The British party stayed in the road, refusing to bow. Samurai struck, slashing Richardson, his blood soaking the earth.[1s] The killing sparked outrage, leading Britain to demand reparations. When Japan stalled, the Royal Navy bombarded Kagoshima months later. The incident forced Japan to open, accelerating the Meiji Restoration.[0.8s]
