In the middle of the Pacific, a lone coral ring lies forgotten.[0.5s] In 1906 Mexico sent soldiers, families, and a lighthouse to claim it. Every two months a ship brought food, water, and hope. Then the Mexican Revolution stopped the ships, leaving the colony starving.[1s] Scurvy spread, men fell, women watched helplessly as life withered. Governor Arnaud launched a desperate boat, but the sea swallowed it. Only Victoriano Álvarez remained, seized the guns, and crowned himself king. For two years he ruled with rifle, rape, and relentless cruelty. Finally, two women smashed him with a hammer as a warship appeared.[0.8s]
