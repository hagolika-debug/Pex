August 18, 1908, the Maypole Colliery erupted in deadly fire. Seventy‐five miners vanished beneath clouds of coal dust. Only three workers survived, hidden in a distant seam. The blast shattered the headgear, silencing the pit forever.[1s] Rescue crews fought flames, but fire forced them to flood the mine. [2s]The last bodies weren't recovered until 1917, years later. King Edward VII sent a telegram, but no words could replace the loss. A memorial stands today at St John’s, honoring the forgotten heroes. Remember the Maypole disaster, a silent echo of industrial peril.
