A tiny nation born by a map error in 1441.[1s] Two surveyors chose different streams, leaving a strip unclaimed. Three hundred fifty farmers declared independence, paying no taxes to anyone. Cospaia had no army, prisons, or written legal code. They grew tobacco freely, defying papal bans and excommunication.[0.5s] Merchants smuggled salt and goods through the duty‐free corridor. In 1826 the Pope split the land, ending the free republic.[1s] Today the hamlet honors its liberty with an annual festival.
