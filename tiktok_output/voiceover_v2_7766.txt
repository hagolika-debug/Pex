In 1900, New York’s streets erupted in a forgotten race riot. A police officer, Robert Thorpe, was killed by Arthur Harris. Harris fled, but the city’s tension ignited a violent mob. Eighth Avenue turned into a battlefield.[1s]Clubs flashing, crowds screaming. Police joined the mob.[1s]Beating black men without mercy. Hundreds were injured; hospitals overflowed with bruised bodies. [0.8s]The riot ended only after the chief ordered troops. The aftermath sparked protests, petitions to the British consul. This event reshaped Harlem’s growth, fueling a new Black community. Remember the 1900 Hell’s Kitchen riot, a silent scar in history.
