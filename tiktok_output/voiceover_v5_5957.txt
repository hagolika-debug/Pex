In 897 Rome, a dead pope faced a courtroom. Stephen VI ordered Formosus' corpse exhumed from the tomb. The lifeless body was propped on a throne, solemnly. A deacon answered for him, as the crowd gasped. He was accused of perjury, ambition, illegal bishoprics.[1s] The verdict? Guilty. His papal vestments stripped away. Stephen cut off the three blessing fingers, sealing his fate. The corpse was thrown into the Tiber, floating among whispers.[0.8s] Rome erupted, deposing Stephen, who met a grim end.
