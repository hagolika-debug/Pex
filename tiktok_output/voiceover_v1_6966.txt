A forgotten war raged over a tiny Andean province. Tarija's rugged hills became the flashpoint between nations. In May 1837, Governor Rosas declared war on Bolivia. Bolivian troops surged into Jujuy and Salta, igniting fierce skirmishes. The Battle of Montenegro crushed Argentine forces, forcing a retreat. Silence fell over the Andes.[2s]The war lingered without resolution. All the while, Chile fought the Confederation, adding chaos. By April 1839, the confederation collapsed, ending the conflict. Tarija stayed Bolivian.[1s]History barely remembers this border saga. Unearthed battles like this remind us—history hides countless stories.
