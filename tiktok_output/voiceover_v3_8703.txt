On New Year's Day 1915, a quiet Australian town faced war. Two armed men on a camel cart opened fire[0.5s] at a picnic train. Four innocent passengers died, seven more were wounded in seconds. The attackers shouted the Ottoman flag, claiming a holy war.[1s] Police and soldiers chased them across the dusty outback, guns blazing. A fierce gun battle erupted on Cable Hill, echoing across the desert. Both gunmen fell, their lives ending on the same rocky ridge.[0.8s] The tragedy sparked anti‐Turkish riots, internment of enemy aliens, and lasting scar. Today, a heritage plaque marks the site, reminding us of forgotten war.
