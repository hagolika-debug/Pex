On a bleak Scottish isle, a lone lighthouse guarded stormy seas. It first shone bright on December seventh, 1899, warning passing ships. Three dedicated keepers—Ducat, Marshall, McArthur—tended its relentless beam. On December 15, 1900, the light mysteriously went dark.[1s] Relief crew arrived days later, finding empty rooms and a cold fire. An overturned chair, open kitchen door, and two missing oilskins hinted panic.[0.5s] Speculation ranged from rogue waves sweeping them away to eerie supernatural forces. Even a year later, no bodies were recovered—only endless speculation. Today the automated light still glows, keeping the mystery alive.
