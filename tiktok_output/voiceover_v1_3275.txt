In 1919, Portugal briefly split in half. Charismatic officer Paiva Couceiro declared a new kingdom overnight.[0.5s] He seized Porto, hoisting the royal blue‐white flag. Rebels promised to restore the 1826 Constitutional Charter. Republicans mobilized, gathering militia on Monsanto hills.[1s] A brutal clash erupted; dozens fell on both sides.[0.8s] Within weeks, the kingdom crumbled under relentless fire. By February 13, the Monarchy of the North vanished. Historians nickname it Traulitânia, a footnote of revolt. Even nations can fracture, then heal, like sunrise after night.
