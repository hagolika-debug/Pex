In 1945, Soviet schoolchildren gifted a wooden Great Seal to America. What seemed like a patriotic symbol hid a secret listening device. A silent ear inside the emblem.[1s]The bug listened undetected. The passive bug, called 'The Thing', needed no battery, just external signal. It hung in the U.S. embassy Moscow, unnoticed for years. In 1952, CIA engineers finally detected the resonant echo.[0.8s]They captured proof. For seven years, Soviet whispers traveled unnoticed across the Atlantic. The bug was removed, but the lesson lingered: trust can be weaponized. Today, hidden tech hides in everyday objects, echoing that Cold War secret.
