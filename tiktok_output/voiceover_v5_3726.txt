A ship vanished off Taiwan's coast, sparking a deadly clash. The American barque Rover struck a hidden reef[0.5s] on March 12, 1867. Fourteen crew members, including the captain's wife, were ambushed by Paiwan warriors. They fought back, blood soaking the sand.[1s] The U.S. dispatched a naval expedition, but the jungle heat crippled them. A single Marine fell, his rifle silent forever.[0.8s] Diplomacy faltered; the incident sparked the 1874 Japanese invasion of Taiwan. Today, a lighthouse stands where the Rover met its doom. A forgotten tragedy that reshaped Pacific power dynamics.
