In 1754, Windham, Connecticut awoke to an impossible roar. Residents thought French troops or Native war drums threatened their sleepy village.[0.5s] They sent a militia patrol to investigate the mysterious thunderous chorus. At dawn, they discovered...[1s]...thousands of bullfrogs battling in the pond. Historians argue drought forced the frogs into a chaotic mating frenzy. The terrified townsfolk fled, shouting names like Dyer and Elderkin in panic. By sunrise, the pond was littered with dead croakers, silence returned. Windham embraced the battle, placing frog statues on the modern Frog Bridge. A town once terrified by frogs now celebrates the oddest war[0.5s] ever.
