In 1837, a tiny steamboat ignited a diplomatic fire. American rebels used the vessel to supply Canadian insurgents across the Niagara. Canadian militia crossed the border, seized Caroline, and set her ablaze. [0.5s]The burning ship drifted, roaring toward the thunderous Niagara Falls. Only one American, Amos Durfee, died, but rumors claimed dozens. [1s]American newspapers screamed war. Public fury surged. Britain threatened retaliation, bringing two nations to the brink.
