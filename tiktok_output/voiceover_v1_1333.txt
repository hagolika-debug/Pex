In 1900, Blagoveshchensk faced a hidden tragedy. A booming Boxer Rebellion sparked panic among Russian Cossacks. Thousands of Chinese residents were forced toward the Amur River. [1s]No boats, just orders to swim across the icy waters. Cossacks opened fire, turning the crossing into a deadly massacre. Estimates of the dead range from a few hundred to several thousand. [0.5s]The horror was quickly erased from Russian history books. Only a century later did scholars uncover the forgotten bloodshed. Remembering Blagoveshchensk reminds us how silence can hide atrocities.
