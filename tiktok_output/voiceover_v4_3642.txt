In 1184, nobles gathered, not for battle, but a bathroom. King Henry VI called a Hoftag to settle a bitter land feud.[0.5s] The meeting took place in the ancient Petersberg church, Erfurt, Germany. Hundreds of heavy cloaks and armor pressed down the wooden floor. Suddenly, the floor gave way, dropping nobles into a foul cesspit.[1s] Sixty to a hundred aristocrats drowned, crushed, or suffocated by the stench. Miraculously, King Henry and Archbishop Conrad clung to iron rails.[0.5s] The disaster halted the peace talks, but Henry’s rise continued. Centuries later, the Erfurt latrine disaster still shocks historians.[0.8s] Even kings can fall...into a medieval toilet.
