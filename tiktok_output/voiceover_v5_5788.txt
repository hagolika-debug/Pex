In 1861, Assam’s fields boiled over with silent fury. British officials imposed heavy taxes on betel nut and paan. Farmers gathered in Phulaguri, demanding justice for their livelihoods. Peaceful petitions fell mute; anger turned into a thunderous chant.[1s] Colonial troops opened fire, blood staining the riverbanks forever. Among the chaos, Lieutenant Singer fell, shocking the empire.[0.8s] The uprising was brutally crushed, but its echo lingered. Decades later, Phulaguri inspired Assam’s freedom fighters across India.[0.5s] Remember the forgotten peasants, whose silent stand sparked a nation.
