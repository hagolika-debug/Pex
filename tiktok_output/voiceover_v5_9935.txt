In 1932, Australia declared war... [1s] against feathered invaders. Farmers faced endless hordes of emus devouring their precious wheat crops. The Defense Minister sent soldiers armed with Lewis machine guns to the battlefield. On November 2, troops opened fire[0.5s], but the birds scattered like tanks. Emus split into small groups, dodging bullets with astonishing speed. Soldiers ran out of ammo, their dignity the only thing truly destroyed.[0.8s] After weeks, the army withdrew, conceding defeat to the flightless army. Farmers turned to fences, while the Emu War became a legendary farce. Today, the Emu War reminds us: nature can outsmart even the strongest guns.
