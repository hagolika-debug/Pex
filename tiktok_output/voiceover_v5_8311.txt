In 1900, a quiet Michigan quarry held a deadly secret. Workers stored over two thousand pounds of dynamite in a flimsy shanty. On a cold January morning, a single stick slipped. A small blast echoed, then the massive explosion ripped the sky.[1s] The blast shattered windows in Detroit, felt forty miles away.[0.8s] Only one man died, Nelson Burbo, leaving ten grieving children. A clock stopped at 10:33, forever marking the tragedy. The quarry crater remains, a silent reminder of unchecked power. Remember the Sibley Quarry, where a simple mistake changed history.[0.5s]
