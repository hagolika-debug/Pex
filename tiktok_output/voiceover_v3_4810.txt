1883, gold fever sparked a secret city on the Amur River. Russian and Chinese prospectors built a bustling town overnight. At its peak, twelve thousand people lived there. They raised a black‐and‐yellow flag, calling themselves a republic. Law, taxes, even a casino thrived amid the chaos.[0.5s] [2s]But the Qing dynasty soon demanded the miners leave. In 1886, Chinese troops burned the settlement to ashes. [1s]The ghost town vanished, its story hidden for decades. Today, the legend lives on as the 'California on the Amur'.
