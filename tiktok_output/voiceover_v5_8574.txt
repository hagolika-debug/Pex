In November 1918, two brand‐new French minesweepers set sail. Built in Fort William, Ontario, they were meant to clear wartime mines. Named Inkerman and Cerisoles, after historic French battles. Their maiden voyage headed for the Atlantic via the Great Lakes. A sudden November blizzard slammed Lake Superior with 30‐foot waves.[1s] Inkerman and Cerisoles vanished without a trace, 76 sailors lost. No wreckage was ever found, making it the Great Lakes’ biggest mystery. Centuries later, treasure hunters still scour the Keweenaw Peninsula for clues. Will the frozen depths finally reveal the fate of these lost warships?[0.5s]
