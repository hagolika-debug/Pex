In 1834, a forgotten war erupted in Greece's rugged Mani peninsula. Young King Otto, only seventeen, sent 3,500 Bavarian troops to enforce order. His regents demanded disarmament of the iconic stone towers guarding local families. The Maniots saw this as betrayal.[1s] They refused to surrender. In April, rebels gathered in Areopolis, chanting freedom and defiance. Bavarian soldiers entered peacefully.[0.8s] The next dawn, ambush erupted. Four hundred Maniots captured thirty-six Bavarians, holding them in brutal captivity. The clash shattered the myth of invincible Bavarian troops, forcing retreat. Ultimately, the negotiations restored autonomy, but the war left a scar on Greek memory.
