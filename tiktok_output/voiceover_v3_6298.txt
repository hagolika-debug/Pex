In 1957, the Soviet Union hid a deadly nuclear explosion. Deep in the Urals, the Mayak plant processed weapons‐grade plutonium. An underground waste tank overheated, triggering a chemical blast. A towering orange cloud rose[1s], raining radioactive dust over villages. The Soviet government denied the disaster, keeping residents in the dark. Only in 1976[0.8s] did dissident Zhores Medvedev expose the tragedy. Thousands fled, and radiation caused chronic illness for generations. Today, the East Ural Radioactive Trace still stains the landscape. Remember Kyshtym[1s], the nuclear disaster the world almost forgot.
