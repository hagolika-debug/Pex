In 1830, Australian convicts set sail for the impossible.[1s] They hijacked the brig Cyprus, escaping Tasmania's brutal penal colony. The mutineers crossed China, chasing a desperate hope of freedom. On January sixteenth, they anchored at Mugi Cove, Shikoku's coast. Samurai disguised as fishermen inspected the ship, noting an unbearable stench. Cannon fire shattered the hull, yet the crew begged for water.[0.8s] Through gestures they offered gifts, a scarlet coat, and a strange boomerang. Centuries later, scholars decoded samurai diaries, confirming the unlikely landing. It became the first Australian vessel to breach Japan's isolation. A forgotten tale of piracy and diplomacy fades into legend.[1.5s]
