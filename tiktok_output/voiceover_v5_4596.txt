In 1651, a tiny war began between the Netherlands and Scilly.[0.5s] Lieutenant‐Admiral Maarten Tromp demanded reparations from Royalist pirates. When answers failed, Tromp declared war—yet never fired a single shot. Parliamentary forces quickly forced the Royalist fleet to surrender.[1s] The Dutch withdrew, leaving the Isles in a state of uneasy peace. No peace treaty ever signed, so the war technically lingered for centuries. Local legend claimed the conflict lasted 335 years—until 1986. A Dutch ambassador finally signed peace, ending the bloodless war.[0.8s] Today, the Isles celebrate the quirky peace with annual reenactments. A reminder that some wars can end without a single bullet.
