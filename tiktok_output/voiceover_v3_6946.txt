1835, Gulf of Mexico, tensions simmer between Mexico and the United States. Mexican schooner Montezuma captures American merchant Martha[0.5s], sparking outrage. A week later, Montezuma snatches Texan vessel Columbia, escalating conflict. USRC Ingham sails south, officially hunting slavers, secretly to free seized ships. In Gulf waters, Ingham confronts Montezuma.[1s] Cannons fire, yet no casualties. Mexican crew deliberately grounds Montezuma, sparing her from American capture. Ingham returns triumphantly, inspiring the future Coast Guard motto 'Semper Paratus'. Today, this silent duel fades from textbooks, a forgotten spark of war.
