In 1938, the U.S. Army Air Corps pulled off a daring stunt Three B‐17 Flying Fortresses set out to find a ship at sea The target? The luxurious Italian liner SS Rex.[1s] Sailing 620 miles offshore Navigators used dead‐reckoning and radio fixes to chase a moving point Storm clouds gathered.[0.5s] The bombers pressed on through rain and hail At 12:23 pm, one pilot shouted, 'There it is!' The B‐17s flew alongside the Rex, waving to stunned passengers Live radio broadcast sent the historic moment coast‐to‐coast, shocking the nation The feat proved bombers could strike far from shore, reshaping air power
