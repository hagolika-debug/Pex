In 1859, a single pig ignited a border crisis. American farmer Lyman Cutlar shot the roaming hog, demanding payment. [1s] The British Hudson's Bay Company claimed the pig was theirs. Negotiations failed, troops from both nations landed on San Juan Island. [0.5s] General Pickett raised cannons, while British warships hovered nearby. No shots fired, yet tensions simmered like a pot about to boil. [2s] President Buchanan sent Winfield Scott to negotiate peace. The dispute ended with joint occupation until 1872 arbitration. Today, the island hosts a park, reminding us of the absurd war.
