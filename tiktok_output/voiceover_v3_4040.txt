In 1925, a tiny border dog started a crisis. A Greek soldier chased his stray across the Bulgaria–Greece line. Bulgarian sentries fired, killing the soldier and a captain. Greece demanded apology, money, and two million francs. When Bulgaria hesitated, Greece invaded the town of Petrich. [1s] Ten days of skirmishes left dozens dead. The League of Nations stepped in, ordering a cease‐fire. [0.8s] Greece withdrew, paying £45,000 in compensation. History remembers this absurd conflict as the War of the Stray Dog.
