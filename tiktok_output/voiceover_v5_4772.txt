Imagine a wave of beer flooding London streets in 1814.[1s] At the Horse Shoe Brewery, massive wooden vats held fermenting porter. A slipped iron hoop caused a 22‐foot vat to burst spectacularly. Hundreds of thousands of gallons surged, creating a 15‐foot beer tsunami.[0.5s] The roaring liquid smashed walls, drowning eight residents in the slums. Families mourned amid broken bricks and frothy wreckage.[2s] Spectators paid to view the disaster, turning tragedy into a grim spectacle. The flood ended wooden vats, ushering concrete tanks across breweries worldwide. A beer disaster still bubbles beneath London's history, waiting to be told.
