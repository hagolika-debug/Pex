In 1741, a tiny Tamil harbour changed colonial history forever. King Marthanda Varma faced the powerful Dutch East India Company. He unified small states, demanding pepper trade freedom. The Dutch blockaded Colachel, then landed troops on the sand. Local Mukkuvar fishermen created fake cannons, confusing the invaders. A stray cannonball ignited[1s] a powder keg, blowing the stockade. Dutch surrendered within days, their empire weakened on Indian soil. Varma imprisoned the commander, then made him his army's admiral. This forgotten victory reshaped regional power, yet textbooks ignore it[0.8s].
