A battle fought after the war officially ended. May 24, 1815, Missouri's silent sinkhole becomes a war zone. Missouri Rangers, unaware of peace, chase Black Hawk's warriors.[0.5s] The Sauk retreat into a deep limestone pit, turning defense into nightmare.[1s] Rangers craft a moving wooden shield, risking death for a shot. Gunfire echoes as warriors fire through holes, killing a ranger.[0.8s] As darkness falls, Black Hawk leads his men out, evading capture. Both sides suffer losses; the war's end remains a distant dream. Forgotten by history, this sinkhole battle shaped the future Black Hawk War.[1.2s]
