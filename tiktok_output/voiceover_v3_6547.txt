In 1184, noble guests gathered for a court day in Erfurt. The crowded hall's rotten floor gave way beneath their combined weight. They tumbled through the floor... [1s] into a stinking cesspit below. About sixty nobles perished, crushed or drowned in human waste. King Henry VI watched[0.5s], barely escaping from a stone alcove. The disaster halted the land dispute, but the dead were forgotten. Centuries later, the tale became a grim local legend. Imagine[0.8s] a party ruined by a collapsing toilet floor.
