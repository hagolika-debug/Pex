In the middle of the Pacific, a tiny coral ring waited. Mexico sent soldiers, families, and a lighthouse to claim the island. The Mexican Revolution halted supply ships, leaving the colony isolated.[1s] Without fresh food, scurvy and hunger claimed the men. Governor Arnaud chased a phantom ship, but his boat capsized. Victoriano Álvarez, the lone lighthouse keeper, seized guns and proclaimed king.[2s] Two brave women struck him with a hammer.[1s] Ending his reign. A U.S. Navy gunboat appeared on the horizon, rescuing the survivors. Clipperton remains a silent reminder of isolation's dark potential.
