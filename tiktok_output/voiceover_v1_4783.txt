In 1184, powerful nobles gathered in Erfurt to settle a feud. They met inside the ancient church's wooden hall, unaware of danger. The floor creaked, then gave way beneath the crowd.[1s] Nobles plummeted into a foul cesspit, screams echoing through stone. Many drowned in liquid excrement, others crushed by falling timbers. King Henry clung to iron rails, barely escaping death.[0.8s] The disaster claimed up to a hundred aristocrats, reshaping imperial politics. Survivors fled, the dispute abandoned, history remembered only for the toilet tragedy. Centuries later, archaeologists uncovered the collapsed floor, confirming the grisly tale. A reminder that even the mighty can fall... into the depths.[1s]
