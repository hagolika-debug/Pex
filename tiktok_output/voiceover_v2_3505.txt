On July 26, 1932, the German training ship Niobe vanished. A sudden white squall rose without warning over the Baltic Sea. All hatches were open, the wind tore the vessel apart. Crew scrambled, but the capsizing was instantaneous. [1s]Forty rescued by a passing cargo ship. Ninety‐three souls were lost to the cold, unforgiving water. [0.8s]The nation mourned, flags at half‐mast for days. A memorial coin financed the new training ship, the Gorch Fock. Today, the Niobe wreck rests as a solemn reminder beneath the waves.
