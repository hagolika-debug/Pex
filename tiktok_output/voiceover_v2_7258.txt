In 1839, a border dispute sparked a war over honey. Missouri claimed the fertile strip with buzzing bee hives. Iowa settlers protested, chopping down three honey trees. A Missouri sheriff tried to collect taxes, was arrested. Both sides mobilized militias,[1s] but no bullets flew. Cold rain and snow turned the standoff into a soggy fiasco. Negotiators met in Waterloo, Missouri,[0.8s] sealing an armistice. The conflict earned the odd name:[1.2s] The Honey War. Today, the border stands, a sweet reminder of a peaceful showdown.
