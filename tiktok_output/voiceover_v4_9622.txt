In 1900, Michigan witnessed a hidden tragedy no one remembers. The Burt Lake Band lived peacefully on their treaty‐protected island. A greedy land speculator claimed the land was delinquent. Sheriff Ming and deputies arrived, orders to burn every cabin. Flames rose.[1s] Families fled with nothing but memories. Only the 1838 St. Mary's Church survived the inferno. Decades later, the Band sued,[0.5s] but courts denied restitution. Today, the story remains a scar on Michigan’s history. Remember the forgotten burn‐out,[1s] lest history repeat itself.
