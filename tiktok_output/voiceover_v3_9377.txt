In 1788, an Austrian army vanished into chaos. They marched toward Caransebeș, seeking Ottoman troops. A hussar spotted locals selling schnapps.[1s]Greed sparked trouble. Infantry demanded the drink, hussars refused, tension rose. A single shot rang out.[0.5s]Chaos erupted instantly. Soldiers shouted 'Turci! Turci!' believing Ottoman attack. Commands echoed, 'Halt! Halt!' misheard as 'Allah! Allah!'.[1.2s]Confusion deepened. Friendly fire consumed the camp; Emperor Joseph II fled. When the real Ottoman army arrived, they found only carnage.
