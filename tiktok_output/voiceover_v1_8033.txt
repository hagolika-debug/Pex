A solitary coral ring, hidden 1,080 km from Mexico's coast. In 1905 Mexico established a military colony, hoping for guano profit. But the Mexican Revolution halted supply ships, leaving them starving.[0.5s] Only one man remained: lighthouse keeper Victoriano Álvarez, alone on the atoll. He seized the rifles, proclaimed himself king, and claimed the island. His reign turned brutal—rapes, murders, fear gripping the women.[0.8s] Two brave women, armed with a hammer, plotted his deadly downfall. [1s]The hammer swung, Álvarez fell, his tyrannical rule shattered. Days later, the USS Yorktown rescued eleven survivors, ending the nightmare. Now Clipperton lies empty, only crabs scuttle across its silent sands.
