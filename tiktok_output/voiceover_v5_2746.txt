In 1885, a remote desert clash threatened global powers. Afghanistan's frontier was a vague line across endless steppe. Russian troops advanced, seizing the Afghan fort of Panjdeh.[1s] Britain, fearing a Russian route to India, prepared for war. Diplomats met, tensions rose, but bloodshed escalated.[0.8s] On March 30th, Russian cannons thundered; Afghans fled. The battle ended, Russia kept Panjdeh, Britain backed down. The crisis forced the great powers to draw Afghanistan’s modern border. A forgotten flashpoint that almost sparked a world war.[1s]
