April 14, 1561, Nuremberg awoke to an impossible sky battle. A printed broadsheet by Hans Glaser captured the strange aerial clash. Witnesses described hundreds of glowing spheres darting, colliding, then exploding.[1s] Among them, a massive black spear pierced the heavens, pointing eastward.[0.5s] People feared divine wrath,[0.8s] believing the sky signs warned sinners. Modern scholars suggest sun dogs and temperature inversions created the illusion. The woodcut inspired later UFO lore, even appearing in Jung’s 1958 book. Today, this 16th‐century sky war remains a hidden chapter of history.
