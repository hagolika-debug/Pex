A forgotten nation sprang up between the U.S. and Canada.[1s] The 1783 Treaty of Paris left the border unclear. Settlers were taxed twice, so they declared independence. In 1832 they wrote a constitution and elected leaders. Luther Parker became their first representative, a true frontiersman. For three years they resisted skirmishes with both nations.[0.5s] American militia occupied the republic in 1835, ending autonomy. The 1842 Webster‐Ashburton Treaty finally drew the line.[0.8s] Today, historic markers whisper the story of Indian Stream.
