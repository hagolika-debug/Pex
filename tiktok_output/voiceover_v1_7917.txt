In 1932, Australia declared war on emus.[1s] Farmers watched feathered giants devour their wheat harvests. The government dispatched army troops armed with Lewis machine guns. Emus scattered like tanks, sprinting across endless golden fields. First volleys killed only a few, birds kept fleeing. Bullets jammed, soldiers ran out of ammo, dignity shattered.[0.5s] After weeks of futile shooting, the army withdrew, defeated. The emus survived, becoming icons of stubborn resilience. Today, the Emu War reminds us nature can outsmart armies.[1s]
