On February ninth, 1913, the heavens performed a silent parade. Forty to sixty fireballs stretched across the horizon, lingering five minutes. No radiant point—just a line of glowing giants, baffling astronomers worldwide. People thought the world was ending.[2s]They stared upward, breathless. Thunderous booms echoed as fireballs roared, shaking houses across Canada. Clarence Chant studied reports, proposing a temporary Earth satellite as source. A second wave appeared before dawn.[1s]The sky repeated its eerie dance. Centuries later, the 1913 procession remains a celestial mystery, rarely remembered.
