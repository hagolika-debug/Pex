In 1883, miners forged a wild gold kingdom on the Amur. Russian and Chinese prospectors clashed, building a bustling frontier town.[0.2s] They called it Zheltuga, the 'California on the Amur'. At its peak, twelve thousand miners lived in lawless luxury. Gold glittered, but violence surged.[1s]Every night, whips cracked. A black‐and‐yellow flag fluttered over hotels, casinos, and a circus. The Qing Empire finally noticed, demanding the illegal settlement vanish. Soldiers arrived.[0.5s]The town burned, its gold lost forever. Today, Zheltuga lives only in dusty archives and legends.
