Port of Spain boiled over when water rates skyrocketed in 1903. Citizens demanded free water, calling it a basic human right. The colonial governor banned public council entry, demanding tickets. That spark ignited a furious protest that night. Crowds pelted the Red House with stones, flames licking walls.[1s] British troops opened fire, echoing across the city streets. Sixteen dead, dozens wounded; the city burned in chaos. The riot forced the government to rethink water policies.[0.5s] Today, the rebuilt Red House stands as a reminder of forgotten fury.
