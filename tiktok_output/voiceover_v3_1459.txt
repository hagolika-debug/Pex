In 1930, Taiwan erupted in a hidden uprising. Japanese colonial rule forced indigenous Seediq into harsh labor.[0.8s] A police insult at a wedding banquet ignited fury. Seediq warriors stormed the school, killing 134 Japanese officials. Japanese troops responded with overwhelming force, deploying mustard gas.[1s] The press was silenced, and thousands of Seediq perished. The tragedy forced Japan to rethink its aboriginal policies.[0.5s] Today, the Musha Incident inspires films and a call for remembrance. A hidden chapter of resistance, echoing through Taiwan’s mountains.
