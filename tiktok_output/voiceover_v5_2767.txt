A tiny nation hid between the US and Canada for three years. The 1783 Treaty of Paris left the border unclear and contested. Both countries sent tax collectors, sparking anger among settlers. On July 9, 1832, they declared independence.[1s] A new republic was born. They drafted a constitution and elected a local assembly. New Hampshire militia gathered, demanding surrender.[0.5s] Tensions rose dramatically. By August 5, 1835, the Republic capitulated and rejoined New Hampshire. The border finally settled in 1842 by the Webster‐Ashburton Treaty. Today a marker in Pittsburg, NH tells the story.[0.8s] Remember the lost nation.
