In 1657, a rumor about a miracle boy sparked terror. A humble farmer whispered the story over tea in Kōri.[0.5s] Officials in Nagasaki heard and launched a secret investigation. Without proof, they ordered a sweep of the entire district. Six hundred hidden Christians were seized, families torn apart. Four hundred and fifty were beheaded; the rest faced endless imprisonment.[1s] An eleven‐year‐old girl spent sixty‐four years confined, never freed. The crackdown shattered the Tokugawa’s belief they’d eradicated Christianity.[0.8s] Yet hidden believers survived, their secret faith echoing through centuries.
