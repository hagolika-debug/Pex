Deep within Hells Canyon, miners chased whispers of hidden flour gold. In May 1887, thirty‐plus Chinese laborers paddled upstream.[0.5s] Their hopes surged. They camped on Deep Creek's banks, isolated by sheer cliffs. Nearby horse thieves plotted revenge, weapons hidden among pine shadows. At dawn, gunfire erupted.[1s] Blood stained river, silence followed. Only one miner fled briefly, then was brutally slain. Weeks later bodies washed ashore, sparking a half‐hearted investigation. A confession emerged, yet the trial declared all perpetrators innocent. For a hundred years, the tragedy faded.[0.8s] Until hidden files resurfaced. Today, a granite marker honors the forgotten souls of Deep Creek.
