Deep in Hells Canyon, 1887, Chinese miners sought hidden gold. They camped by the remote Deep Creek, far from civilization.[0.5s] Two white horse‐thieves plotted, driven by greed and hatred. One night, gunfire shattered the silence.[1s] The creek ran red. Thirty‐three miners fell, their bodies later washed downstream. The thieves stole the flour gold, then vanished into the canyon. Authorities dismissed the tragedy, leaving the victims forgotten for decades.[1s] In 1995, hidden files resurfaced, revealing the massacre's truth. A memorial now stands, honoring those silenced souls. Their story reminds us history can hide in the deepest canyons.
