Imagine a tiny nation hidden between the U.S. and Canada. It was called the Indian Stream Republic, 1832‐1835. A vague border from the 1783 Treaty sparked the conflict.[0.8s] Double taxes drove locals to draft their own constitution. They elected a council, with Luther Parker as Justice. New Hampshire militia marched in.[1s]The tiny republic trembled. By August 1835, Indian Stream was absorbed into New Hampshire. The 1842 Webster‐Ashburton Treaty finally settled the border. Today, a historic marker commemorates this forgotten micro‐nation.[0.5s]
