In 1838, rebels tried to spark a Canadian revolution from a windmill. American volunteers crossed the St. Lawrence, believing Canadians would rise. They seized the stone windmill at Prescott, turning it into a fortress. But locals sounded alarms.[1s]The militia mobilized instantly. British cannons battered stone walls, yet the mill barely crumbled. Rebels expected reinforcements.[0.5s]None arrived, hope faded. After days of siege, the invaders surrendered under relentless fire. Leader Nils von Schoultz was hanged, his dream crushed. Today the windmill stands, a silent reminder of a forgotten battle.
