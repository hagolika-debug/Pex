In 1184, nobles gathered in Erfurt for a crucial meeting. They never expected the floor to collapse beneath them.[1s] The wooden beams gave way, dropping them into a foul cesspit. Some nobles drowned in liquid excrement, others were crushed. The stench was so lethal, a few suffocated instantly. King Henry VI and Archbishop Conrad miraculously clung to iron rails. Estimates say sixty to a hundred aristocrats perished that day. The disaster halted the political dispute, but power shifted upward.[0.8s] A medieval toilet tragedy reminding history that even rulers can fall.[0.5s]
