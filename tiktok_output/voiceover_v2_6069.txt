In the wild Atlantic, tiny Flannan Isles guard the sea. A white stone lighthouse rose in 1899, shining through fog. Three keepers—Ducat, Marshall, McArthur—tended the light each night. [0.8s]December winds howled, battering cliffs like giants. When relief arrived, the lighthouse stood empty—no men, no footprints. Doors shut, beds unmade, a single oilskin lay on the floor. Investigators believed massive wave surged up, sweeping the men from the cliffs. [1.5s]No bodies ever recovered, only endless speculation. The vanished keepers inspire songs, poems, and chilling midnight tales.
