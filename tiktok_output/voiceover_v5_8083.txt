In 1838, rebels stormed a stone windmill on the St. Lawrence. They hoped to spark a Canadian uprising.[0.5s] Against British rule. But locals sounded alarms, and militia forces quickly mobilized. Inside the windmill, rebels fortified themselves behind thick stone walls. British artillery bombarded the tower, yet the walls absorbed most shots. Supplies dwindled, reinforcements never arrived, and hope faded.[1s] By November 16, the British launched a final overwhelming assault. The rebels surrendered; leaders were executed or exiled to Australia. Today, the windmill stands as a silent reminder of a bizarre, forgotten war.[0.8s]
