In 1904, the Russian fleet roamed the North Sea. They believed Japanese torpedo boats hid among the fishing trawlers. Fog rolled over Dogger Bank, obscuring all silhouettes.[1s] Suddenly, searchlights pierced the night, targeting harmless vessels. Russian guns roared, shells exploding into the trawlers' nets. Chaos erupted[0.8s] as Russian ships fired on each other too. Two British fishermen died, six wounded; a Russian priest fell. The incident almost sparked war between Britain and Russia. Today, a monument stands in Hull, remembering the tragic mistake.
