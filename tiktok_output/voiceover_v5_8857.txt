On July 24, 1915, Chicago’s river turned into a nightmare. The massive Eastland steamer, overloaded with workers, began to list.[1s] Hundreds of families boarded, unaware the ship was a ticking time bomb. Within minutes, the vessel tipped like a turtle, crushing decks. Water flooded in, panic erupted; screams echoed across the river.[0.8s] Rescue boats scrambled, but lifeboats were tangled on the overloaded decks. Over 800 souls perished, many children, their futures erased forever. The tragedy vanished from history, eclipsed by Titanic’s fame.[1.2s] Today, the Eastland rests beneath Chicago’s waters, a silent reminder.
