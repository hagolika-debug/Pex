In 1776, a tiny Caribbean island changed history forever. Statia's Dutch fort raised a cannon salute for the new American flag. Jewish merchants secretly shipped gunpowder to George Washington's army. British Admiral Rodney called the island a 'nest of vipers'. The salute echoed across the harbor.[2s]The world barely noticed. In 1781, Rodney's fleet seized Statia, ending its trade. Jewish families were expelled.[1s]Their legacy vanished. Today, a museum in Philadelphia honors the forgotten first salute.
