A sleepy Welsh village[1s] faced a storm that would claim six heroes. On February 1st, 1903, the lifeboat launched at dusk. Coxswain Tom Rogers led fourteen brave men into the raging sea. They set out to free the stranded SS Christina near Port Talbot. The lifeboat capsized three times, throwing men into icy, furious waters[0.8s]. Bowman Sam Gammon dove again, saving three comrades despite exhaustion. Six crewmen perished, trapped against the unforgiving breakwater. Mumbles mourned; a solemn funeral procession honored the fallen heroes[1.2s] on February 5th. A brass plaque still stands, reminding us of their sacrifice.
