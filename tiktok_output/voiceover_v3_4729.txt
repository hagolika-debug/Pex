On New Year's Day 1919, a quiet island faced disaster. Hundreds of Lewis men returned home after the Great War's end. They boarded the naval yacht Iolaire, hoping for a safe arrival.[1s] Misty night, faulty navigation, and overloaded decks set the stage. At Stornoway harbor, the vessel struck hidden rocks, splintering instantly. Only seventy‐nine survived; over two hundred lives were lost forever. The tragedy sank silently into history, forgotten by most. [0.5s] Today, a lone memorial stands, honoring those lost. [1s] Remember the Iolaire, a reminder of fragile peace.
