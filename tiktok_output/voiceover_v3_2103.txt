In 1830, a band of convicts fled Tasmania's prisons. They seized the brig Cyprus, charting a desperate escape. Sailors battled storms, reaching China before daring westward turn. A Japanese samurai, disguised as fisherman, spotted the foreign hull.[1s] The ship anchored at Mugi Cove, pleading for water and firewood. Samurai demanded surrender; cannons roared, shaking the brig's deck. Negotiations stalled; the crew signaled for three more days.[0.8s] Finally, winds shifted, the Cyprus slipped away under night’s cover. History finally confirmed: the first Australian ship set foot in Japan.
