Imagine a secret Swedish colony thriving where America was born.[1s] In 1638, Peter Minuit led 25 settlers to the Delaware River. They called it New Sweden, stretching across present-day Pennsylvania, Delaware, New Jersey. It introduced the log cabin, shaping the American frontier forever. The Dutch discovered the squatters.[0.8s] Tensions rose. In 1655, Peter Stuyvesant's fleet forced surrender without a single shot. Swedish descendants voted for independence, influencing the 1776 Declaration. Today, Old Swedes' Church still holds services, a living relic. The forgotten colony proved even tiny nations can shape a continent.
