1962, Tanganyika: a school erupts in uncontrollable laughter. Within weeks, nearby villages caught the giggling contagion. Teachers tried discipline, but laughter only grew louder. Students convulsed, fainted, some cried amid hysterics. Doctors called it mass psychogenic illness.[1.5s]The cause remained a mystery. Authorities quarantined schools, yet laughter echoed through fields. Songs and jokes spread, but fear lingered behind smiles. We still wonder.[2s]What truly sparked the laughter? Decades later, the epidemic shows emotions can infect entire societies.
