In 1741, a tiny fishing village halted a European empire. King Marthanda Varma of Travancore challenged the Dutch East India Company. The Dutch blockaded Colachel, aiming to crush pepper trade. Local Mukkuvar fishermen sabotaged Dutch ships under moonlit waters. A lucky cannonball struck a powder barrel, causing massive explosion.[1s] Dutch troops, out of ammunition, were forced to surrender. De Lannoy, the captured admiral, became Travancore’s chief military engineer. The victory ended Dutch dominance on India’s Malabar Coast. Today, a stone pillar stands in Colachel, remembering the forgotten triumph.[0.8s]
